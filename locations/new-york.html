Why New York businesses choose Funnels That Print

  • Dense competition means the first reply wins. In a crowded metro like New York City, a prospect can find ten competitors in a minute. Our speed-to-lead automation makes sure you are the first to call back.
  • Clicks are expensive, so waste hurts. We verify phone numbers and qualify in the funnel, and send booked appointments back to Meta through the Conversions API, so the algorithm learns from buyers rather than form fills.
  • Multi-location clinics. Our New York med spa client had three clinic locations. The funnel routes each booking to the right clinic calendar and reminder sequence.
  • Show rate is where the money is. Three-channel reminders lift show-up rates as high as 88%, which matters most when each slot is costly.

Frequently asked questions.

Do you have an office in New York?

No. Funnels That Print has a single office in Dubai, UAE, and serves New York clients remotely. We meet over video, share live dashboards and report weekly in writing. The New York med spa, injury and home service results on this page were all delivered without a local office.

Is lead generation more expensive in New York City?

Ad costs in dense metros like New York City are usually higher than in smaller markets, because more businesses compete for the same audience. That makes qualification and follow-up more important: verified phones, instant callbacks and reminders turn expensive clicks into appointments instead of abandoned forms.

Can you handle a med spa with several New York locations?

Yes. Our New York med spa client ran three clinic locations. The funnel asks which location suits the client, books into that clinic's calendar, and sends reminders with the right address. Reporting shows revenue and bookings per location so you can move budget toward the clinic that converts best.

What are OTP-verified injury leads?

The claimant confirms their phone number with a one-time code before the lead is submitted. That removes fake numbers and accidental entries. Our New York MVA campaign delivered 276+ verified calls in 5 days, so intake staff spent their time on real accident victims instead of dead numbers.

How quickly can a New York campaign launch?

Builds go live in 5 to 10 business days after the kickoff call. Pricing is a one-time $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and paid directly by you.
