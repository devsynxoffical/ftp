AI appointment setter · Insurance

An AI appointment setter for insurance agents and agencies

An AI appointment setter for insurance calls every new final expense, life, Medicare or auto and home lead within a minute, confirms the basics, and books a call or appointment with a licensed agent who is licensed in that lead's state. It never quotes, never recommends a policy and never pretends to be an agent. Its job is to make sure the licensed producer's time goes to people who are ready to talk.

Quick answer

How does an AI appointment setter work for insurance leads?

The AI calls and texts each new lead within about 60 seconds, says it is an AI assistant, confirms the person requested information, checks state, age range, coverage type and a few non-medical fit questions, then books a time with an agent licensed in that state. Pricing, health underwriting and plan advice stay with the licensed agent.

01

Why insurance leads need a first call in seconds

Insurance leads, especially from Meta and shared vendors, are often contacted by several agencies within the hour. The agency that reaches them first and books a specific time usually gets the conversation. In our deployments the AI reaches a median first contact of 38 seconds and lifts lead-to-booking 2.4x on the same lead flow. Our AI voice agents page covers the underlying technology, latency and handoffs.

Detailed health questions belong in the agent's application process, not the AI call. At most, the AI asks whether the lead is comfortable discussing health on the agent call. Our lead qualification questions template shows how to keep the list short.

03

Booking with licensed agents by state

The agent reads the lead's state and books only from producers licensed there. Agencies with captive and independent agents can route by carrier appointment too. Each booking carries the answers, the lead source and a short summary, and the producer receives an SMS alert with a one-tap link to the record. Unanswered leads get a retry after 10 minutes, a second retry after two hours and a text with two time options, all logged in the CRM.

04

Compliance boundaries we build in

  • TCPA consent. AI-voice calls to mobile numbers need prior express written consent in the US. We review form wording and keep the consent record and timestamp on the contact.
  • AI disclosure. The first line states that the caller is an AI assistant working for your agency.
  • No quoting by AI. The agent cannot give prices, compare carriers, discuss benefits or recommend a plan. Those questions trigger a booking with a licensed agent.
  • Medicare rules. CMS marketing rules restrict unsolicited contact and require the scope of a sales appointment to be agreed and documented in advance, with timing requirements of their own. We keep Medicare calls to scheduling with consented leads and leave the scope of appointment to your agents and compliance team.
  • State rules and quiet hours. Calling windows and do-not-call scrubbing are set per state.

We set up the workflow; your compliance officer or counsel signs off on scripts before launch.

05

Cost per booked insurance appointment

Running costs are typically $0.10 to $0.25 per minute, about $3 to $9 per booked appointment, compared with $40 to $120 when a human setter's pay is divided by bookings. With final expense leads costing $7.79 each in one documented campaign, the setter's cost per booking is small next to the lead cost it protects. Frequently asked questions.

Can the AI quote premiums or recommend a policy?

No. We configure it to decline any request for prices, carrier comparisons or plan recommendations and to offer a time with a licensed agent instead. Quoting and advice require a license and depend on underwriting details the AI should not collect, so keeping it to scheduling protects both the consumer and your agency.

Does it work for Medicare leads?

It can schedule calls with Medicare leads who have consented to be contacted, but Medicare marketing is tightly regulated and the rules change. We keep the AI to confirming interest and booking a time, and your compliance team decides the scope-of-appointment process and wording before any Medicare script goes live.

How does it handle health questions?

It avoids them. Final expense and life underwriting depends on health history, which is sensitive and belongs on the licensed agent's call. The AI may ask whether the person is comfortable discussing health with the agent, and if they volunteer details it notes that they want to discuss health and moves on to booking.

Can it route leads to agents licensed in the right state?

Yes. Each producer's calendar is tagged with the states they are licensed in, and the AI offers slots only from matching producers. Agencies can add carrier appointments or language as extra routing rules, so a Spanish-speaking final expense lead in Texas reaches the right person first time.

What if a lead says they never asked to be contacted?

The AI apologizes, confirms the opt-out, ends the call and marks the contact do-not-call in the CRM so no further calls or texts go out. We also flag the lead source, because repeated complaints from one vendor usually point to weak consent capture that should be fixed upstream.

What does setup cost for an insurance agency?

The AI setter is part of our $1,999 complete suite with CRM, automation and tracking, delivered in 5 to 10 business days including test calls. After launch you pay the voice platform and telephony directly, usually $0.10 to $0.25 per minute of talk time.
