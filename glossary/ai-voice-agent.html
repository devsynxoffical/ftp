Quick answer

What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is an automated caller that talks like a person. It listens, understands, replies in under a second and takes actions such as booking a slot or updating the CRM. In funnels it handles first contact and qualification at roughly $0.10 to $0.25 per minute, around the clock.

How an AI voice agent works

  1. A new lead triggers a webhook from the CRM.
  2. The agent calls within seconds, introduces itself as an AI assistant and confirms the enquiry.
  3. Speech is transcribed, the language model decides the reply from an approved script and knowledge base, and a synthetic voice speaks it.
  4. Qualified leads are offered two specific slots and booked on the live calendar.
  5. The outcome, answers and a summary are written back to the CRM pipeline.

Because it calls instantly at any hour, it is the most common way we deliver sub-minute speed to lead.

04

Common mistakes

  • Hiding that it is AI. Disclose it in the first sentence; trust and compliance both depend on it.
  • Slow replies. Latency over a second makes calls feel robotic and raises hang-ups.
  • No knowledge base. An agent that cannot answer simple questions loses the lead.
  • No human handoff. Let callers reach a person when they ask.
  • Calling without consent. Collect call and text consent on the form and respect calling hours.

Frequently asked questions.

Do AI voice agents sound like real people?

Modern agents sound natural, with realistic voices and replies in under a second. Callers can usually still tell it is AI over a longer conversation, which is why we disclose it at the start. In practice, prospects care more about fast, useful answers and easy booking than about who is calling.

How much does an AI voice agent cost?

Running costs are typically $0.10 to $0.25 per minute of talk time. With normal contact and booking rates that comes to roughly $3 to $9 per booked appointment, compared with $40 to $120 per booking from human setters. Setup and scripting are separate one-time costs.

Can an AI voice agent replace my sales team?

No. It replaces the repetitive first-contact work: calling new leads, qualifying, booking, confirming and rebooking no-shows. Your closers keep the sales conversation and spend their time only on leads who already meet your criteria, which usually makes the team more productive, not smaller.

What can an AI voice agent connect to?

Most agents connect by webhook or API to CRMs such as GoHighLevel and HubSpot, to calendars for live booking, and to SMS for follow-up texts. They write call outcomes, transcripts, answers and booked times back to the contact record so your team sees everything.

Is it legal to call leads with an AI voice agent?

It can be, when the lead consented to calls and texts on your form, the agent discloses that it is AI, and you respect calling hours, do-not-call lists and opt-outs. Rules vary by country and state, so have scripts and consent language reviewed before launch.
