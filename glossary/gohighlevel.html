What is GoHighLevel? The GHL CRM explained simply

GoHighLevel (also called HighLevel or GHL) is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform. It combines a CRM, funnel and website builder, email and SMS marketing, calendars, automation workflows, phone calling and AI assistants in one account. It was built for marketing agencies, which can white-label it for clients, and it is now widely used by local service businesses and coaches.

What is GoHighLevel used for?

GoHighLevel is used to capture, follow up and convert leads in one place. Businesses build funnels and forms, store contacts in the GHL CRM, send automated SMS and email, book appointments into calendars and track deals in a pipeline. Agencies use it to run many client accounts and resell it under their own brand.

01

GoHighLevel in one minute

Before platforms like GoHighLevel, a typical small business paid for a funnel builder, an email tool, an SMS tool, a scheduling app, a CRM and sometimes a call tracking service, then tried to connect them. GoHighLevel replaces that stack with one login and one database, so a form submission can trigger a text, an email, a pipeline move and a reminder sequence instantly. That speed is why it is popular for lead generation, where contacting a lead within a minute matters (see speed to lead).

02

How the GHL CRM works

  1. Capture: a lead fills a form, books a call, messages on Facebook or calls a tracking number. A contact is created automatically.
  2. Follow-up: a workflow sends an instant SMS and email, assigns the lead to a rep and can start an AI conversation.
  3. Pipeline: the lead becomes an opportunity in a CRM pipeline, moving through stages such as contacted, booked, showed and won.
  4. Booking: calendars book appointments and send reminders that reduce no-shows (see show rate).
  5. Reporting: dashboards show leads, appointments and revenue by source, and events can be sent to Meta through the Conversions API.

Frequently asked questions.

Is GoHighLevel a CRM?

Yes. GoHighLevel includes a full CRM with contacts, tags, custom fields, pipelines and opportunities. Unlike a CRM-only tool, it also includes funnels, websites, SMS, email, calendars, calling and automation, which is why people often call it an all-in-one marketing platform rather than just a CRM.

What is the difference between GoHighLevel and HighLevel?

None. HighLevel is the company and product name, and GoHighLevel comes from its original domain. GHL is the common abbreviation. The GHL CRM, HighLevel CRM and GoHighLevel CRM all refer to the same platform.

How much does GoHighLevel cost?

As listed in November 2026, Starter is $97 a month, Unlimited is $297 a month and the agency plan with SaaS mode is $497 a month. Usage for SMS, calls, email and AI is billed on top, and some features such as a branded mobile app are paid add-ons.

Is GoHighLevel good for small businesses?

Yes, for small businesses that sell through calls or appointments, such as local services, clinics and coaches. It replaces several tools and makes instant follow-up simple. It is less suited to businesses that only need email marketing or a simple website, where cheaper tools are enough.

Is GoHighLevel hard to learn?

It has a steep learning curve because it does so much. Most teams only use a few screens daily: the inbox, the pipeline, the calendar and contacts. A clean setup with clear stages and named workflows makes it easy for staff, which is why many businesses hire a specialist for the initial build.

Can GoHighLevel replace ClickFunnels?

For lead generation funnels, yes, and it adds SMS, calendars and a CRM. For complex info product funnels with many upsells, ClickFunnels is still more polished. Our ClickFunnels alternatives comparison covers when each one fits.
