What is speed to lead? It is the time between a lead submitting a form, calling or messaging, and your business making first contact. In paid lead generation it is one of the strongest predictors of whether a lead ever answers, books and buys.

Quick answer

What is speed to lead?

Speed to lead is the elapsed time from the moment a lead converts to the moment a real person or AI agent first contacts them by phone or text. Faster is better: across our 60+ deployments, contacting leads inside a minute lifted lead-to-booking rates 2.4x compared with the same ads before automation.

01

How speed to lead works

  1. A form submission triggers an automation in the CRM the moment it arrives.
  2. An SMS goes out instantly and an AI voice agent or round-robin call to a salesperson starts within seconds.
  3. If there is no answer, the workflow retries by call and text at set intervals.
  4. The outcome is logged and the lead moves to the right stage in the CRM pipeline.

The automation runs nights and weekends, which is where many human-only teams lose leads.

02

How to measure it

Speed to lead = time of first contact attempt - time of form submission

Report it as a median, not an average, because a few overnight leads distort averages. Track two companion metrics: the share of leads contacted inside 60 seconds, and the contact rate, meaning leads who actually answer. Log all three in your funnel KPI dashboard.

04

Common mistakes

  • Email as first contact. For service leads, a call and text in the first minute beat an email by a wide margin.
  • Business hours only. Leads from evening ads wait until morning and have often booked a competitor.
  • One attempt. Many leads answer on the second or third try, so build a retry sequence.
  • Measuring averages. Use the median and the share contacted inside 60 seconds.

Related terms

Frequently asked questions.

What is a good speed to lead time?

Under 60 seconds for paid leads. Our median across 60+ deployments is 38 seconds, with 84% of leads reached inside the first minute. Anything over five minutes is slow for inbound leads, and leads contacted hours later answer far less often, especially in competitive industries such as legal and home services.

Why does speed to lead matter so much?

Because interest fades fast and competitors call too. A lead who filled in a form is thinking about the problem right now. Calling while they are still on their phone means they answer, remember you and book. A few hours later, many will not pick up an unknown number.

Can speed to lead be automated?

Yes. A CRM workflow can text the lead instantly and start a call within seconds, either connecting a salesperson by round-robin or using an AI voice agent to qualify and book. Automation also covers nights and weekends, when human teams usually cannot respond.

Does speed to lead matter for low-ticket offers?

Less so. For a $27 product, the purchase usually happens on the page and follow-up is by email. Speed to lead matters most where the lead must speak to someone before buying: local services, legal, mortgage, insurance, high-ticket coaching and B2B services.

What tools do I need for speed to lead?

A CRM that triggers instantly on new leads, such as GoHighLevel, an SMS and calling provider, and either a sales team on round-robin or an AI voice agent. Tracking is needed too, so you can measure time to first contact, contact rate and booking rate.
