Order bump: definition, pricing and examples

An order bump is a small, related offer placed on the checkout page itself, usually as a checkbox above the payment button. The buyer adds it with one click before paying, so it raises average order value without adding a step to the funnel.

Quick answer

What is an order bump?

An order bump is a one-click add-on shown on the checkout page before payment, such as a template pack, an extra module or a bonus item. It is priced below the main product, complements it directly and is accepted by ticking a box, which raises average order value with no extra page.

How an order bump works

  1. The buyer reaches checkout for the main product, often a tripwire offer.
  2. A highlighted box near the payment button describes one add-on in two or three sentences.
  3. Ticking the box adds it to the same payment.
  4. After payment, the buyer sees upsells and downsells on separate pages.

Because the bump sits on a page every buyer sees, even a modest take rate has a large effect on whether the front end becomes a self-liquidating offer.

02

Example: a $17 bump on a $27 product

In our $847K low-ticket funnel the main product cost $27 and the order bump cost $17. The bump was something that made the main product faster to use, not a separate topic. Together with upsells, the checkout recovered 112% of ad spend on day zero.

Order value formula: average order value = main price + (bump price x bump take rate) + upsell revenue per buyer.

Illustration: with a $27 product, a $17 bump and a 30% take rate, the bump adds $5.10 to every order, raising the front end to $32.10 before upsells.

04

Common mistakes

  • An unrelated bump. The add-on should finish or speed up the main product.
  • Too many bumps. One clear bump outperforms three competing checkboxes.
  • Pricing it above the main product. High-priced add-ons belong in the upsell.
  • Weak copy. The bump box needs its own headline and a single specific benefit.

Frequently asked questions.

What is a good order bump take rate?

Take rates vary with fit and price, but a well-matched bump priced at about half the main product often converts a meaningful share of buyers. Track it as bumps sold divided by orders. If fewer than one in ten buyers accept, rewrite the copy or change the offer before changing the price.

How much should an order bump cost?

Price it below the main product, commonly 30 to 70% of the front-end price. Our $847K funnel paired a $27 product with a $17 bump. A bump that costs more than the main product feels like a second purchase decision and usually belongs on an upsell page instead.

Which platforms support order bumps?

ClickFunnels, GoHighLevel, Systeme.io, Kajabi and most Shopify checkout apps support them, though the setup differs. We often use ClickFunnels for product checkouts with bumps and upsells, and GoHighLevel when the buyer also needs CRM, email and text follow-up in the same account.

Do order bumps hurt checkout conversion?

A single, relevant bump rarely lowers checkout conversion in our experience, because it does not add a page or a decision before the payment button. Several bumps, a confusing layout or a bump that looks pre-ticked can hurt trust and conversion, so keep it simple and opt-in.

What makes a good order bump offer?

The best bumps complete the main product: templates for a course, a done-for-you version of a worksheet, a fast-start video or an extra use case. Buyers should read it and think it makes their purchase work better. Avoid unrelated bonuses or generic memberships.
