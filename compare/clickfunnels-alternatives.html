ClickFunnels alternatives: 6 funnel builders compared honestly

Looking for a ClickFunnels alternative? The right one depends on what happens after someone opts in. If you sell through calls, you need SMS and a calendar. If you sell courses, you need a course platform. If you sell physical products, you need a store. We build funnels on most of these tools, and this is how they compare.

Quick answer

What is the best ClickFunnels alternative?

GoHighLevel is the best ClickFunnels alternative for businesses that sell through booked calls, because it adds a CRM, SMS and calendars to the funnel builder. Systeme.io is the best budget option, Kajabi is best for courses, and Shopify is the better choice for physical products. ClickFunnels itself is still strong for info products and upsells.

The verdict

Pick GoHighLevel if leads need follow-up by phone and text before they buy. Pick Systeme.io if price is the deciding factor. Pick Kajabi if the product is a course or membership. Pick Shopify for physical products. Stay on ClickFunnels if you sell info products with order bumps and upsells and your follow-up is mainly email.

Funnel features that matter more than the builder

Switching builders rarely fixes a funnel that does not convert. These elements decide results on any platform:

  • The offer and headline above the fold, matched to the ad that brought the visitor.
  • Form friction: multi-step forms and quizzes often beat one long form. See our quiz funnels.
  • Revenue per buyer: an order bump and a one-click upsell can lift average order value without more traffic.
  • Follow-up speed: for calls, contacting a lead inside a minute matters more than any page design. That is speed-to-lead automation.
  • Tracking: purchase and lead events sent through the Conversions API so ads optimize on real outcomes.

Frequently asked questions.

What is cheaper than ClickFunnels?

Systeme.io is the main cheaper alternative, with a free plan and paid plans from about $27 a month, compared with $97 a month for ClickFunnels Launch. It covers funnels, email and courses well for solo creators, but it lacks SMS, calling and advanced automation.

Is GoHighLevel better than ClickFunnels?

For businesses that sell through calls or appointments, usually yes, because GoHighLevel adds the CRM, SMS, calendars and automation that ClickFunnels lacks, at a similar starting price. For info products that rely on complex checkouts, bumps and upsells, ClickFunnels is still the more polished funnel builder.

Can I move my ClickFunnels funnels to another platform?

There is no one-click export between platforms, so pages are rebuilt. Copy, images and structure carry over, and a rebuild is a good moment to fix weak pages. Export contacts and customer lists, recreate products and payment settings, then point your domain and ads at the new funnel.

Which ClickFunnels alternative is best for courses?

Kajabi is the strongest for courses, memberships and community, while Systeme.io is the budget option with courses included even on lower plans. ClickFunnels and GoHighLevel can host courses too, but course delivery is not their core strength.

Do I need a funnel builder at all?

Not always. A local service business may only need one strong landing page with instant follow-up, and a store may only need Shopify with a good product page. A funnel builder pays off when you run several offers, tests or upsell paths, or need forms, pages and automation in one place.
