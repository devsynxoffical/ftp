GoHighLevel vs HubSpot: which CRM fits a lead generation business?

GoHighLevel vs HubSpot is a choice between an all-in-one lead generation account and a deeper, more structured CRM. We build most booked-call funnels in GoHighLevel and integrate HubSpot when a sales team already lives in it. Here is the verdict, the November 2026 pricing and where each one genuinely wins.

Quick answer

Is GoHighLevel or HubSpot better?

For small and mid-sized businesses selling through booked calls, GoHighLevel is better value: funnels, SMS, calendars and automation in one flat-priced account. HubSpot wins for larger sales teams that need cleaner reporting, deal management, forecasting and enterprise integrations, and it costs considerably more once you move past the Starter tier.

01

The verdict

Pick GoHighLevel if your funnel ends in a booked call, you have fewer than about ten people working leads, and speed-to-lead by SMS and phone matters more than reporting polish. Pick HubSpot if you run a multi-stage B2B sales process with several reps, need forecasting and attribution your leadership trusts, or depend on integrations such as Salesforce-style deal workflows, enterprise SSO or a large app ecosystem. HubSpot is the more mature CRM; GoHighLevel is the faster and cheaper lead generation machine.

03

Where HubSpot wins

HubSpot is easier for a new rep to learn, its data model is cleaner, and its reporting is the better tool for answering "which campaign produced revenue." Sequences, meeting links, deal forecasting and the integration marketplace are mature. If you already pay for HubSpot and your team uses it daily, moving to save on software is rarely worth the disruption. The free CRM is also a real option for a founder logging a few deals.

04

Where GoHighLevel wins

GoHighLevel puts the funnel, form, CRM pipeline, SMS, email, calendars, reminders and review requests in one account at a flat price. That makes 60-second speed-to-lead one workflow rather than an integration project, and across 60+ deployments that follow-up produced a median first contact of 38 seconds and 2.4x lead-to-booking. Agencies can also clone a full setup as a snapshot for each client.

05

Who should pick which, and how we use them

  • Agencies, roofers, med spas, coaches: GoHighLevel. See our GoHighLevel agency service.
  • B2B SaaS and service firms with five or more reps: HubSpot as the system of record, often with a GoHighLevel or custom front end feeding it.
  • Already on HubSpot Starter and outgrowing it: compare the Professional upgrade cost against a GoHighLevel build before deciding.

For clients we build the funnel and CRM automation in whichever system the sales team will actually use. When HubSpot stays, we push leads into it by webhook and keep SMS follow-up and booking reminders running. Frequently asked questions.

Is GoHighLevel cheaper than HubSpot?

Usually, yes, once you need more than the free or Starter tier. As of November 2026 GoHighLevel Starter is $97 a month with SMS and email usage billed separately, while HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional runs around $890 a month with three seats plus an onboarding fee. HubSpot Starter is cheap for a small team, but per-seat costs grow with headcount.

Can GoHighLevel replace HubSpot?

For most small lead generation businesses, yes. It covers contacts, pipelines, email, SMS, calendars and automation. It is a weaker replacement for teams that rely on HubSpot's forecasting, detailed attribution reporting, quotes or enterprise integrations. In those cases we usually keep HubSpot as the record and add GoHighLevel or custom automation for follow-up.

Can I use GoHighLevel and HubSpot together?

Yes. A common setup runs the funnel, SMS follow-up and booking reminders in GoHighLevel and syncs qualified contacts and booked appointments into HubSpot, where the sales team manages deals. The sync needs clear rules about which system owns each field, otherwise duplicates and overwritten data appear within weeks.

Which is easier to learn?

HubSpot. Its interface is cleaner and new reps pick it up quickly. GoHighLevel has more features in one place but a steeper learning curve and a less polished interface, which is why many businesses hire a specialist for the initial setup and then train the team on the few screens they use daily.

Which has better reporting?

HubSpot, clearly. Its attribution, deal forecasting and custom reports are stronger and easier for leadership to trust. GoHighLevel's dashboards cover pipeline, appointments and source data well enough for most small businesses, and we pair them with Conversions API tracking so the ad platforms see booked calls and sales.

Does HubSpot have a free plan?

Yes. HubSpot's free CRM tools cost $0 and suit a founder or small team logging contacts and deals. Paid features such as automation sequences, removal of HubSpot branding and more seats start at the Starter tier. GoHighLevel has no permanent free plan, only a trial, but includes automation at every paid tier.
