ClickFunnels vs Kajabi: checkout engine or course platform?

ClickFunnels vs Kajabi is really a question about what earns your money. ClickFunnels is built to sell: order bumps, one-click upsells and fast funnel pages. Kajabi is built to deliver: courses, communities, coaching and memberships with a polished student experience. Here is the verdict and November 2026 pricing.

Quick answer

Is ClickFunnels or Kajabi better?

ClickFunnels is better for selling, especially low-ticket front ends with order bumps and one-click upsells that need to recover ad spend fast. Kajabi is better for delivering courses, memberships and communities with a clean student experience. Many creators use ClickFunnels to sell and Kajabi to host, which works well if you can manage two systems.

01

The verdict

If your growth depends on paid traffic and front-end conversion, choose ClickFunnels. If your growth depends on retention, community and a product students love using, choose Kajabi. Kajabi's checkout is good enough for organic launches and email sales; ClickFunnels' course area is good enough for a simple product. Neither is a strong CRM for booked-call sales.

03

Where Kajabi wins

Kajabi gives buyers a better place to land. Its course player, community, coaching tools and branded mobile app make a membership feel premium, which helps retention and refunds. It is also simpler to run as one tool for content, email and payments. For a creator selling mostly to an existing audience, Kajabi often beats ClickFunnels outright.

04

Where ClickFunnels wins

On cold traffic, the checkout decides whether ads pay for themselves. Our $847K low-ticket funnel used a $27 product with a $17 order bump and one-click upsell and recovered 112% of ad spend on day zero. That depends on a fast, flexible checkout and quick split tests, which is ClickFunnels' strength. Its funnel templates also make it faster to test new front ends.

05

Who should pick which, and how we use them

  • Paid-traffic low-ticket offers: ClickFunnels checkout, with buyers synced into a CRM for the backend.
  • Course or membership with an existing audience: Kajabi.
  • Both: sell in ClickFunnels, grant access in Kajabi by integration.
  • High-ticket offers sold on a call: neither as the core system; use a VSL funnel on GoHighLevel.

For clients we build the front end where conversion is highest, then connect buyers to CRM automation for upsell calls and reactivation. Check whether a front end self-liquidates with the ROAS calculator and read the low-ticket funnel guide.

Frequently asked questions.

Is Kajabi more expensive than ClickFunnels?

Yes, at list price. As of November 2026 Kajabi starts at $179 a month billed monthly, about $143 on annual billing, while ClickFunnels Launch is $97 a month. The gap narrows if you would otherwise pay for separate course hosting, community software and an email tool alongside ClickFunnels, since Kajabi includes them.

Can I use ClickFunnels and Kajabi together?

Yes. A common setup sells the front end and upsells in ClickFunnels, then grants course access in Kajabi through a native integration, Zapier or a webhook. It gives you the stronger checkout and the better student experience, at the cost of two subscriptions and two places where customer data lives.

Which converts better on paid ads?

In our experience ClickFunnels, because its checkout, order bumps, one-click upsells and split testing are built for cold traffic. Kajabi pages convert well for warm audiences that already know you. For ad-driven low-ticket funnels the upsell flow is often what makes the campaign profitable, so checkout quality matters more than page design.

Does Kajabi have funnels?

Yes. Kajabi includes pipeline-style funnel templates for opt-ins, webinars and product launches, with order bumps and upsells. They are easier to set up than ClickFunnels funnels but offer less control over layout, step logic and testing. For a creator selling to an email list they are usually enough.

Which is better for high-ticket coaching?

Neither is ideal as the main sales system. High-ticket coaching sells on a call, so you need booking calendars, SMS follow-up, reminders and a pipeline, which GoHighLevel handles better. Kajabi is a good place to deliver the coaching program after the sale, and ClickFunnels can host a VSL page, but the follow-up belongs in a CRM.
