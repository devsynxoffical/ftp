GoHighLevel alternatives: 7 options and when each one wins

The best GoHighLevel alternatives depend on why you want to leave. Some teams want a cleaner CRM, some only need funnels, some sell courses, and some find the usage fees and learning curve too much. We build on GoHighLevel and on several of these tools, so here is an honest comparison, including when switching is not worth it.

Quick answer

What is the best alternative to GoHighLevel?

HubSpot is the best alternative for B2B sales teams that need strong reporting. ClickFunnels suits funnel-first info product sellers, Systeme.io is the budget pick for solo creators, Kajabi fits course businesses, ActiveCampaign is strongest for email automation, and Vendasta targets agencies reselling local marketing. For booked-call lead generation, GoHighLevel is still hard to beat on value.

The short list

Pick by the job, not the hype.

  • HubSpot

  • ClickFunnels

The verdict

If your business sells through booked calls, such as local services, coaching or agencies, GoHighLevel remains the best value because the funnel, CRM, SMS, calendar and automation sit in one account. Switch when a different job matters more: HubSpot for B2B reporting and multi-rep sales, Kajabi for course businesses, ClickFunnels for funnel-first info products, Systeme.io when budget is the main constraint, and ActiveCampaign when email is your main channel.

03

Reasons people leave GoHighLevel, and the fix

  • "It is too complicated." Usually a setup problem, not a platform problem. A clean build with a few well-named pipelines and workflows is easy to use daily. That is what our GoHighLevel agency service delivers.
  • "Usage fees keep growing." Check for runaway workflows and duplicate messages first. For agencies, rebilling usage to clients through white label SaaS mode fixes the margin.
  • "Reporting is weak." True compared with HubSpot. Send pipeline events to Meta through the Conversions API, or sync deals into HubSpot if leadership needs deeper reports.
  • "I only use the funnel builder." Then a simpler, cheaper tool may genuinely fit better. See ClickFunnels alternatives for funnel-first options.

05

How to switch without losing leads

  1. Export contacts, tags, pipeline stages and opportunity values first.
  2. List every active workflow and what triggers it, so nothing silently stops.
  3. Rebuild the follow-up before moving traffic: forms, reminders and speed-to-lead messages.
  4. Port or replace phone numbers and re-register them for A2P texting where needed.
  5. Run both systems in parallel for one to two weeks and compare bookings.

Frequently asked questions.

What is cheaper than GoHighLevel?

Systeme.io is the cheapest serious option, with a free plan and paid plans from about $27 a month. HubSpot's free CRM costs nothing for basic contact management. Both do far less than GoHighLevel for lead follow-up, though: neither includes native SMS, calling and calendar automation at the same depth.

Is HubSpot better than GoHighLevel?

For B2B teams with several reps who need forecasting and attribution, yes. For small businesses that sell through booked calls, GoHighLevel usually gives more for less, since funnels, SMS, calendars and automation are included at a flat price. Our full GoHighLevel vs HubSpot comparison covers pricing and features.

Is ClickFunnels a good GoHighLevel alternative?

Only if your business is funnel-first, such as info products, low-ticket offers or webinars. ClickFunnels is strong for checkouts and upsells but has no native SMS or full CRM for working leads by phone. Many of our clients use ClickFunnels for the sale and GoHighLevel for follow-up.

What do agencies use instead of GoHighLevel?

Agencies mostly compare GoHighLevel with Vendasta for reselling local marketing products, and with HubSpot's partner program for B2B clients. GoHighLevel remains the most common choice because snapshots, sub-accounts and SaaS mode let one team run and resell many client systems from a single login.

Should I leave GoHighLevel?

Leave if your main job fits another tool better: courses, email-only marketing or complex B2B reporting. Stay if your complaint is complexity or messy automations, because a cleaner setup usually fixes that for less than a migration costs. A short audit of your account answers the question quickly.
