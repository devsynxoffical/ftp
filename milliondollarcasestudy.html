About the $1.52M build

Frequently asked questions.

What funnel generated the $1.52M result?

A high-ticket consulting offer sold through an institutional-grade VSL and application funnel, with dynamic qualification filters placed before the sales call. The $1,520,000 was collected over 19 months from cold paid traffic. The filters kept unqualified prospects off the calendar, so closers spent their time on buyers who matched the offer's budget and situation.

What follow-up automation was used?

Multi-touch email and SMS follow-up triggered the moment a lead applied, plus a reminder sequence before each booked call. The reminder stack lifted show-up rates from 62% to 88.4%. Applicants who did not book received further follow-up, so interested prospects who hesitated on their first visit still had a path to a call.

How was ad tracking set up for this funnel?

Meta Conversions API with offline conversion events for qualified calls and closed deals, so the ad algorithm optimized for buyers rather than form fills. Cost per booked call fell from $280 to $112. Sending downstream outcomes back to Meta is what let the campaign keep improving as spend increased instead of getting worse.

Can the same stack be built for my offer?

Yes, if you sell a high-ticket service, coaching or consulting offer through a sales call. Builds take 5 to 10 business days. Book a strategy call and we will map the funnel, automation and tracking to your offer, using this case as the blueprint and adjusting the qualification questions to your buyers.

How much does a high-ticket funnel like this cost to build?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. A stack like this one corresponds to the complete suite. Ad spend is separate, and there is no revenue share on what the funnel sells.
