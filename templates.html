How to use the templates together

The templates follow a lead from first click to closed deal. The copy and VSL templates get the opt-in. The SMS, email and AI voice templates handle the first minutes and days, which is where most funnels leak: across 60+ speed-to-lead deployments, contacting leads inside a minute lifted lead-to-booking 2.4x on the same ads. The reminder sequence protects booked calls, and the qualification questions keep closers on buyers. The KPI dashboard ties it together so you can see where the next fix belongs.

Pair the dashboard with our free funnel calculators to put a dollar value on each gap, and see the sales funnel glossary entry for the stage names we use.

Frequently asked questions.

Are these sales funnel templates really free?

Yes. Every template downloads as a plain-text file with no sign-up or email gate. Copy it into your funnel builder, CRM, Google Docs or voice platform and edit freely. They are the same starting documents we use on client builds, adjusted to remove anything specific to one client.

Which platforms do the templates work with?

All of them. The files are plain text, so they paste into GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, HubSpot, Klaviyo, Google Sheets, or any AI voice platform. Placeholders in braces, like {first_name}, map to the merge fields your software uses. Swap the brace syntax for your platform's version before sending.

Do I need to customize them?

Yes. The structure is proven, but the words must match your buyer, offer and industry. Replace every placeholder, rewrite examples in your customers' language, and add real proof. Each template page includes notes on how to adapt it for local services, legal, finance, coaching and eCommerce.

Are the SMS and voice templates compliant?

They include consent language, opt-out instructions such as Reply STOP, and AI disclosure lines, which are good practice. Compliance also depends on how you collected consent, when you send and where your leads live. Rules vary by state and country, so review your setup with counsel before launch.

Can you set the templates up for me?

Yes. Our full suite build includes the funnel pages, follow-up sequences, reminders, an AI voice booking agent and tracking, customized to your offer and connected to your CRM. Most builds go live in 5 to 10 business days. Book a strategy call to see which pieces you need.
