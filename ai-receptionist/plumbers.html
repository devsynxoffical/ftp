01

Why do plumbers miss so many calls?

Because the person who answers the phone is usually the person doing the job. A plumber with wet hands under a sink, in a crawl space or on a water heater install cannot pick up, and most small plumbing businesses do not have a full-time office. Calls that go to voicemail at those moments are often the most valuable ones: emergencies that need someone today and will call down the list until someone answers.

An AI receptionist answers every call, every time, and turns each one into a booked job, a transfer or at least a logged lead with a callback task. Pair it with speed-to-lead automation so web and ad leads get an instant text too.

02

How does it handle plumbing emergencies?

It follows emergency rules you set. When a caller reports a burst pipe, flooding, a sewage backup, no water at all or a leaking water heater, the call is marked urgent. The receptionist gives approved steps, such as turning off the main water valve or the water heater's supply, then transfers to your on-call plumber. If nobody picks up, it books the earliest emergency slot, texts the customer a confirmation and alerts you by SMS.

  • Gas water heater with a gas smell: the caller is told to leave and call the gas utility or 911 first. The AI never troubleshoots gas.
  • Active leak or flood: shut-off guidance, then transfer or the first emergency slot.
  • Routine work: dripping taps, slow drains and quotes are booked into normal hours.
03

What does the plumbing call flow look like?

  1. Greeting with your business name and an AI disclosure.
  2. Problem: leak, clog, no hot water, installation, inspection or an existing job.
  3. Urgency check: is water running now, is there sewage, is anyone without water?
  4. Location: address and ZIP code checked against your service area and any after-hours call-out zone.
  5. Booking or transfer: emergency transfer, or the next arrival window for the job type.
  6. Follow-up: a text confirmation, an optional link to send photos, and a call summary in your CRM.
04

Can it quote prices and call-out fees?

Yes, if you want it to, and only the prices you approve. Most plumbers let the receptionist quote the standard call-out or diagnostic fee and the after-hours rate, and explain that a firm quote is given on site. It never invents a price. For larger jobs such as repipes, water heater replacements or sewer lines, it books an estimate visit instead of quoting.

05

Will it work with my phone number and job software?

Yes. You keep your existing number and forward unanswered or after-hours calls to the receptionist, or give it a dedicated number for ads. We build the booking and CRM in GoHighLevel, which syncs with Google Calendar, and can pass jobs to Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan through an integration or webhook. Reminders from our CRM automation cut no-shows on booked estimates.

Frequently asked questions.

Can an AI receptionist handle a burst pipe call?

It handles the first minute: it recognises the emergency, tells the caller how to shut off the main water valve using your approved wording, and transfers the call to your on-call plumber. If nobody answers, it books the earliest emergency slot and texts you the details.

Do I need an office manager to use it?

No. Many of our home service clients are owner-operators. The receptionist answers, books and logs calls on its own, and you get a text summary of each call, so you can stay on the job and still know exactly who called and why.

Will customers mind talking to an AI?

Most care far more about getting through and getting a time booked than about who answers. The receptionist says it is an AI assistant for your business, and anyone who wants a person is transferred or promised a callback.

Can it take a deposit or card payment?

We do not set receptionists up to take card details by voice. If you take deposits, the receptionist can text a secure payment link from your existing payment provider after the booking.

What if a caller is outside my service area?

It checks the ZIP code against your service area. Callers outside it are told politely, and you can choose to have it refer them on, take their details anyway or offer an out-of-area fee.

How quickly can I go live?

Five to ten business days, including at least 50 test calls covering emergencies, quotes, wrong numbers and unclear callers. Your real line is only forwarded once the test calls pass.
