01

Why do real estate agents need an AI receptionist?

Because buyers and sellers call when they are interested, not when you are free. Sign calls come in during showings, listing questions arrive in the evening, and portal leads expect a reply in minutes. An agent who calls back two hours later is often the third agent that person has spoken to.

An AI receptionist answers every call immediately, gives accurate listing details, qualifies the caller and books the next step, so you can spend your time with clients instead of chasing voicemails. For web and portal leads, an AI appointment setter for real estate calls them back within a minute.

02

How does it qualify buyers and sellers?

It asks the same short set of questions on every call, then books the right appointment:

  • Buyers: area, price range, timeline, pre-approval status, and whether they need to sell first. Qualified buyers are offered a showing or a buyer consultation.
  • Sellers: property address, timeline, reason for moving and whether they are already listed. Sellers are offered a valuation call or listing appointment.
  • Renters, vendors and existing clients: routed or logged according to your rules.

Every answer is written to the contact, so your follow-up starts where the call ended.

03

Is it compliant with fair housing rules?

It is designed to be. The receptionist answers every caller from the same approved script and listing details, and it is set up not to ask about or comment on protected characteristics, describe neighbourhoods in terms of who lives there, or steer callers toward or away from areas. You and your broker approve the script before launch, and call summaries are kept so you can review conversations. You remain responsible for compliance, so we build the script with your brokerage's policies in mind.

04

Does it work with my real estate CRM?

Yes. We build on GoHighLevel by default, with calendar sync for showings, round-robin across agents, and long-term nurture for leads who are six to twelve months out. If your team runs on Follow Up Boss, kvCORE or another real estate CRM, we pass the contact, call summary and booked appointment across through an integration or webhook. Long-term leads can be woken up later with a database reactivation campaign.

05

Which calls should still go to a person?

Offers, negotiations, contract questions and anything emotional or legal belong with an agent. The receptionist is set up to recognise those calls and transfer them, or book an urgent callback when no one is available. It also never gives legal, tax or mortgage advice; it books the caller with the right professional instead.

06

How much does an AI receptionist for real estate cost?

An AI receptionist from Funnels That Print is part of our $1,999 complete suite, which also includes the CRM setup, follow-up automation, AI booking and call tracking. Call minutes are paid by you to the voice platform and usually run $0.10 to $0.25 per minute of talk time, so a 3-minute call costs well under a dollar. Setup takes 5 to 10 business days: we write the call flow and script, load your FAQs and service area, connect your calendar and transfer rules, then run at least 50 test calls before your real line is forwarded.

Frequently asked questions.

Can an AI receptionist answer questions about my listings?

Yes, from the listing details you approve: price, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, HOA, school district names and open house times. If a caller asks something that is not in the details, it says it will check with the agent and books a callback.

Can it book showings?

Yes. It offers showing slots from the listing agent's calendar, respects any showing instructions you set, and texts the buyer a confirmation and reminder. You can require pre-approval before it books a private showing.

Is an AI receptionist the same as an AI appointment setter?

No. The receptionist answers people who call you. An AI appointment setter calls or texts new web and portal leads within a minute of them coming in. Many real estate teams use both, and they share the same calendar and CRM.

Will it work for a whole brokerage?

Yes. Calls can be routed by listing, by area or by round-robin across agents, with separate calendars and an office line for everything else. Brokers see a summary of every call across the team.

Does it handle fair housing correctly?

It answers every caller from the same approved script and is set up not to discuss protected characteristics or steer callers. Your broker approves the script before launch, and you keep a record of every conversation.

How fast can it go live?

Five to ten business days: we write the scripts for buyers, sellers and listing questions, load your listings, connect calendars and transfer numbers, and run at least 50 test calls before your number is forwarded.
