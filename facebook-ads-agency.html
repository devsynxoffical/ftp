01

How we structure a Meta ad account

Most accounts we inherit have too many campaigns, too little budget per ad set and no clean way to tell what is working. We simplify:

  • One testing campaign where new creative and angles compete on a fixed budget.
  • One scaling campaign with broad targeting that only receives proven ads.
  • Retargeting for page visitors, video viewers and form openers who did not finish, sized to the account rather than run by default.
  • Optimization on the deepest event Meta can learn from: qualified lead or booked call for lead generation, purchase with value for eCommerce, sent through the Conversions API.

Broad targeting works when the creative does the targeting. That is why most of our effort goes into what people see, not into interest lists.

02

Creative testing: UGC, static and video

Creative is the biggest lever in a Meta account. We test in rounds, changing one variable at a time so the winner tells us something:

  1. Angle first. Price, speed, trust, outcome or fear of loss. The angle decides who stops scrolling.
  2. Format second. UGC-style talking videos, simple static images with a strong headline, and short demo or before-and-after videos.
  3. Hook third. The first line or first two seconds, rewritten until click-through rate holds at scale.

In one live roofing ads audit, a creative set reached an 18% click-through rate. For eCommerce, the same loop runs on product angles and offers, and the results are collected on eCommerce case studies.

04

Instant forms with OTP or a landing page?

Meta instant forms are fast and cheap but attract more low-intent leads. Landing pages filter better but cost more per lead. We choose per offer:

  • OTP-verified instant forms when volume and speed matter. A one-time code sent to the phone confirms the number is real before the lead reaches your CRM. A Texas mortgage client received 823 OTP-verified leads this way.
  • Landing pages, quizzes or estimators when the buyer needs context or qualification first. We build these in-house through our landing page design service.

We compare both in depth in Facebook lead ads vs funnels.

05

Scaling rules and reporting on cost per booked call

We scale on rules agreed in advance, not on a good afternoon. Budgets rise in steps while cost per booked call or ROAS stays inside target; ads that fall outside it for several days are paused; new creative enters through testing, never straight into scaling.

Reporting follows the money: spend, leads, contact rate, booked calls, shows, closed deals and revenue. A lead campaign at $5 per lead with a 10% booking rate is worse than one at $15 with a 40% rate, and only a report that reaches the booking shows that. Leads are called inside a minute through speed-to-lead automation, and you can benchmark your numbers with the cost per lead calculator.

06

Pricing and who pays for ads

Ads management is quoted on a call after we see your account, market and goals; we do not publish a fixed management fee. Your ad spend is always paid by you directly to Meta from your own ad account, so you keep full ownership of the account, data and pixel history. Funnel and landing page builds are separate one-time projects. Book a call to get a quote and an honest view of whether Meta ads fit your offer.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does your Facebook ads management cost?

Management is quoted on a call because it depends on the number of campaigns, creative volume, industry and whether we also build the funnel. We do not publish a fixed fee. Ad spend is separate and is paid by you straight to Meta from your own ad account, never through us.

How quickly will I see leads?

Most lead campaigns produce first leads within days of launch. A Texas roofing client received 686 leads at $5.31 each in eight days. How long it takes to reach a stable cost per booked call depends on budget, market and offer, and we report it weekly while creative and offers are tested and Meta's algorithm learns from your conversion events.

Do you run ads for eCommerce brands too?

Yes. For eCommerce we run creative testing, offer and bundle tests, and scaling on purchase value with the Conversions API. Our documented store results include $2.3M in sales at 11.6x ROAS and many brands scaled at 4x to 5x ROAS, all listed on the eCommerce case studies page.

Instant forms or a landing page: which is better?

Neither wins everywhere. Instant forms with OTP phone verification give volume at a lower cost per lead, while landing pages and quizzes give higher intent. We usually test both against cost per booked call, because the cheaper lead is not always the cheaper customer.

Who owns the ad account and creative?

You do. Campaigns run in your Meta Business Manager and ad account, billed to your card, and the creative we produce is yours to keep. If you stop working with us, the account, pixel data, audiences and ads stay with you and keep running if you choose.

Can you work with a strict ad category like mortgage or insurance?

Yes. Credit, housing and some financial offers fall under Meta's Special Ad Categories, which limit age, gender and detailed targeting. We work within those rules using broad delivery, compliant creative and offers that qualify the buyer on the form or page instead of in the targeting.
