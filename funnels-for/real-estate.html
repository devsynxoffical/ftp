Funnels for real estate agents

A real estate lead generation funnel that separates buyers, sellers and browsers

A real estate lead generation funnel has to cope with the longest and messiest buying cycle of any industry we serve. Most people who click a listing ad are months away from moving. The funnel's job is to find the ones who are ready now, keep the rest warm, and get both to an agent without the agent spending hours on cold calls. Quick answer

How should a real estate lead funnel be structured?

Split it into a seller path and a buyer path. Sellers get a home value estimate in exchange for address and timeline. Buyers get a curated listings or first-time buyer guide and answer questions about price range, area and pre-approval. Both feed a CRM that calls hot leads instantly and nurtures the rest for months.

Real estate lead generation: the main sources compared

Agents and teams usually mix several lead sources. They differ in what each lead costs you, whether other agents receive the same contact, and how ready the person is to transact.

SourceHow you payIs the lead yours alone?Typical readiness
Portal leads (listing sites)Monthly spend or a referral fee at closingDepends on the programOften active buyers asking about one listing
Sphere, past clients and referralsTime and staying in touchYesHigh, but limited volume
Open housesTimeYesMixed: neighbors, browsers and serious buyers
Google search ads and local SEOPer click, or content over timeYesHigh intent, more competition in big metros
Meta ads into your own funnelPer impressionYesEarlier in the journey, so follow-up decides the result

Meta ads into your own funnel give you the most volume you control, but those leads are often months from a move. That is why the funnel below separates sellers from buyers and runs a long follow-up instead of handing every form to an agent the same day.

The question wording matters because it doubles as the agent's call script. Our lead qualification questions template includes real estate examples.

03

Follow-up for a six-month decision

Hot leads get a call within a minute through speed-to-lead automation, because the first agent to talk to a ready seller usually gets the listing appointment. Everyone else goes into a long nurture built with CRM automation: monthly market updates for their area, new listings that match their answers, and a check-in text every few weeks asking whether their timeline has changed. A lead who says "next year" in March is often ready by August.

04

Cost per lead from our real estate campaigns

From our real estate case studies: 69 leads at $11.57 on $798, 71 leads at $12.12, 247 leads at $16.48 on $4,069, and 157 leads at $28. Seller leads tend to cost more than buyer leads and are worth more to most agents.

05

Fair housing and ad rules

Real estate ads on Meta run under the housing special ad category, which removes age, gender and zip-code targeting and narrows other options. That means the creative and the funnel questions do the targeting. Never write copy that expresses a preference about who should live somewhere, and keep the broker name and license details your state requires on every page.

06

Common real estate funnel mistakes

  • Sending every lead to a single agent who calls once and gives up.
  • Instant home value tools with no human follow-up, which feel like bait.
  • Treating "just browsing" as dead instead of nurturing.
  • No handoff to a lender partner for buyers who are not yet pre-approved.

Frequently asked questions.

How much do real estate leads cost from a funnel?

Our documented real estate campaigns produced leads between $11.57 and $28. Seller leads with a home value hook usually cost more than buyer leads but are worth more because a listing is the more profitable side for most agents. Cost per appointment is the better measure of success.

Do home value funnels still work?

Yes, if the follow-up is human and fast. Homeowners expect an automated estimate to be rough, so the funnel positions it as a starting point and offers a free comparative market analysis from a local agent. Instant tools with no call afterward tend to produce low-quality leads and few appointments.

How long should real estate leads be nurtured?

At least six to twelve months. Many buyers and sellers start researching long before they act. A light nurture with area market updates, matching listings and occasional check-in texts keeps you top of mind, and the CRM moves a lead back to the hot list when they reply or revisit.

Can real estate ads target specific neighborhoods?

Meta's housing special ad category restricts zip-code targeting and requires a minimum location radius. You can still reach a general area, then use creative that names neighborhoods, property types and price points so the right people self-select. The funnel's area question confirms fit before an agent calls.

Should teams use an AI agent for real estate leads?

An AI voice agent is useful for the first contact: calling within seconds, confirming timeline, price range and pre-approval, and booking a call with the right agent. It frees agents from dialing cold leads. Complex conversations about pricing strategy or negotiation should still go to a person.
