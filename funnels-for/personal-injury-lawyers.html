Funnels for personal injury law firms

Personal injury leads from a funnel that delivers signable accident cases

Good personal injury leads are measured by signed cases, not form fills. Motor vehicle accident claimants are stressed, often injured and contacted by several firms. The funnel has to screen for case viability, prove the phone number is real, and put the claimant on the phone with intake while they are still deciding. Quick answer

What does a personal injury lead funnel ask claimants?

When the accident happened, whether they were at fault, whether they received medical treatment, whether police were called, and whether they already have an attorney. Those five answers predict case viability. The phone number is OTP-verified, consent is captured, and intake calls within a minute or the claimant calls in directly.

01

The case qualifier

The quiz is framed as "see if you may have a case" and stays under six questions:

  1. Type of accident (car, truck, motorcycle, rideshare, pedestrian).
  2. When did it happen? Recent accidents are within the statute of limitations and easier to document.
  3. Were you at fault?
  4. Did you receive medical treatment?
  5. Do you already have a lawyer?
  6. Name, state and verified phone number.

Claimants who already have counsel or were clearly at fault get a respectful exit instead of an intake call. Our lead qualification questions template covers wording for each step.

02

Verified calls and intake speed

Personal injury is the clearest case for speed-to-lead automation: the first firm to have a real conversation usually signs the case. Our builds verify the phone by one-time code, then either connect the claimant straight to intake or ring intake staff with the quiz answers on screen. One campaign produced 276+ OTP-verified calls in five days. After hours, an AI voice agent collects accident details and books a callback without giving legal advice.

03

Attorney advertising and consent

Follow your state bar's advertising rules: label ads and pages as attorney advertising where required, do not promise outcomes or settlement amounts, and identify the responsible attorney or firm. Past results need the disclaimers your jurisdiction requires. Automated calls and texts need prior express written consent naming the firm, captured beside the submit button and stored with the lead. Avoid buying shared leads whose consent and origin you cannot document.

05

Buying auto accident leads vs building your own funnel

Firms looking for auto accident leads usually compare three sources:

SourceWho else gets the leadControl over questions and consentSpeed of contact
Shared leads from a vendorSeveral firmsNoneWhoever calls first wins
Exclusive leads from a vendorOnly your firmLimited, and origin is hard to verifyDepends on the vendor's delivery
Your own funnel and ad accountOnly your firmFull: you write the qualifier and consentSeconds, with verified calls

Buying leads is quicker to start. Your own funnel takes 5 to 10 business days to build, but you own the data, the consent record and the ad account history, and you can tighten qualification when signed cases matter more than volume.

06

Where firms lose signable cases

  • Intake that works 9 to 5 while most accident searches happen evenings and weekends.
  • Optimizing for cheap leads instead of signed retainers.
  • No follow-up after the first unanswered call.
  • Creative that sounds like every other firm's billboard.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a personal injury lead cost?

Our documented MVA campaigns ranged from $4.38 to $27 per lead. The $388K revenue case paid $18.94 per lead across 1,079 leads. Cheaper leads are not automatically better; stricter qualification on fault, treatment and representation raises cost per lead and lowers cost per signed case.

Are funnel leads exclusive to my firm?

Yes. Leads from your own funnel and ad account go only to your firm, unlike shared leads sold to several firms at once. You also control the qualifying questions, the consent language and the speed of contact, which are the three biggest drivers of signed cases.

What is an OTP-verified call?

The claimant receives a one-time code by text and enters it before submitting, which proves the number is real and theirs. They are then connected to intake or called immediately. Verified calls remove fake and mistyped numbers, which matters when each conversation is a potential five-figure case.

Can an AI agent do personal injury intake?

An AI agent can make first contact after hours, gather accident type, date, injuries and treatment, and book a callback with intake. It must not give legal advice, evaluate the case or promise outcomes. A licensed attorney or trained intake specialist should handle the substantive conversation.

Which case types work best with this funnel?

Motor vehicle accidents are where our results come from: car, truck, motorcycle and rideshare collisions. The structure adapts to slip-and-fall or workplace injury, but those claimants search and qualify differently, so the questions and creative change. Mass tort campaigns require a separate build.
