A roofing lead generation funnel built to fill the inspection calendar

Quick answer

What does a good roofing lead generation funnel look like?

A three to five question quiz about roof age, damage and homeownership, an OTP-verified phone number, then an instant text and call that books a free inspection. The offer is the inspection or insurance claim help, not a discount. Follow-up runs for 14 days, and booked inspections are sent back to Meta as conversions.

01

Roofing lead generation: where roofers get jobs

Roofing leads come from two very different markets. Insurance work follows storms: hail and wind create a short burst of claims in one area. Retail work is homeowners replacing an aging roof on their own budget. Each channel serves one market better than the other.

ChannelMarketIs the lead yours alone?Watch out for
Storm canvassing and door knockingInsuranceYesOnly works in the weeks after a storm, and needs a crew
Google Local Services and Search adsBoth, mostly repairYesMany calls are small repairs, not replacements
Meta ads into a quiz funnelRetail replacement and storm areasYesNeeds qualifying questions or you pay to inspect renters' roofs
Shared lead marketplacesBothOften noYou compete on speed and price with every other buyer of that lead
Referrals and past customersBothYesRarely enough volume on their own

A free inspection offer on Meta, filtered by homeowner status, roof age and damage type, is the most scalable of these because you control the volume and the leads are exclusive. That is the funnel this page describes, and our roofing case studies show what it has cost.

02

The front end: a quiz, not a contact form

Homeowners do not wake up wanting a roofer. They react to a hailstorm, a stain on the ceiling, or a neighbor's new roof. The ad meets that moment and the funnel keeps the commitment small:

  1. Do you own the home? (filters renters immediately)
  2. How old is the roof? Under 10, 10 to 20, over 20 years.
  3. Any visible damage, leaks or missing shingles after recent storms?
  4. Are you planning to file an insurance claim?
  5. Name, address and mobile number, verified by a one-time code.

OTP verification is the single biggest lead-quality lever we have seen in roofing. It costs some volume but removes fake numbers and accidental submits, which we walk through in the OTP-verified forms strategy session. More question ideas are in our lead qualification questions template.

03

Booking the inspection while the lead is warm

Roofers are on roofs, not at desks, so the follow-up has to run without them. Our speed-to-lead automation texts within seconds and rings the office or rep. When nobody answers, an AI appointment setter calls the homeowner, confirms the address and books a slot on the inspection calendar. Unbooked leads get a 14-day text and email sequence that references their quiz answers, and storm-season reactivation pulls old leads back in.

04

Realistic roofing cost per lead

From our roofing case studies: 537 leads at $4.27, 686 leads at $5.31 in eight days, 635 leads at $6, 1,068 leads at $17.40 and 1,024 leads at $24 on $24,902 of spend. The spread reflects market size, storm timing and how strict the qualification is. Stricter funnels cost more per lead and less per signed contract. Our roofing cost per lead playbook breaks this down further.

05

Roofing funnel mistakes we fix most often

  • Promising "free roof" or guaranteed insurance approval. It inflates volume, angers adjusters and gets ads rejected.
  • Optimizing Meta for raw form fills. Send verified leads and booked inspections back as events instead.
  • Calling back the next morning. By then two other roofers have already been to the house.
  • No service-area filter. Ask for the zip code early and disqualify out-of-area addresses automatically.

The same estimator pattern powers our HVAC, solar and home services funnels. If you are an agency looking for roofing leads rather than a funnel, ContractorLeads supplies verified contractor leads.

Frequently asked questions.

How much should a roofing lead cost?

In our documented roofing campaigns, cost per lead has ranged from $4.27 to $24. Leads under $10 usually come from broad storm-damage offers in active markets; $15 to $25 is typical when the funnel filters harder for homeowners with older roofs. Judge it by cost per signed job, not cost per lead.

Should a roofing funnel use Facebook lead forms or a landing page?

Native lead forms are cheap but attract more accidental submits. A landing page quiz with OTP phone verification costs more per lead and produces far more reachable homeowners. Many roofers run both and compare cost per inspection after 30 days. Our comparison of lead ads and funnels covers the trade-off.

What offer works best for roofing ads?

A free roof inspection or storm-damage assessment, ideally framed around a recent weather event in the area. Insurance claim guidance also works where it is legal and accurate. Avoid promising a free roof or guaranteed approval, which attracts the wrong leads and creates complaints and ad rejections.

How fast do roofing leads need to be contacted?

Within a minute if possible. Homeowners after a storm often submit to several roofers, and the first one to call usually books the inspection. Across our speed-to-lead deployments, median first contact was 38 seconds and lead-to-booking rose 2.4x versus slower manual follow-up on the same ads.

Can an AI agent book roofing inspections?

Yes. An AI voice agent calls new leads instantly, confirms homeownership, address and damage details, and books a slot on your inspection calendar. It works nights and weekends when crews are unavailable. AI calling typically runs about $0.10 to $0.25 per minute, roughly $3 to $9 per booked appointment.
