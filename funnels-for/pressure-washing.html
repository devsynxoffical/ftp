01

Pressure washing leads: where they come from

Pressure washing and power washing companies usually get leads from five places. They differ in whether other companies get the same request, and in how quickly the job can be booked.

SourceIs the lead yours alone?Watch out for
Google Business Profile and mapsYesNeeds reviews and photos before it brings steady calls
Google Local Services and search adsYesCost per click rises in spring and summer
Meta ads into a quote funnelYesNeeds before-and-after footage and a reply within minutes
Shared lead marketplacesOften noSeveral companies call the same homeowner, so price becomes the deciding factor
Repeat customers and neighborsYesOnly works if past customers are reminded to rebook

Owning the lead source matters most in this trade because jobs are small and repeatable: a driveway clean today is a house wash next season. The funnel below is built for exclusive leads from Meta ads, with the instant quote and rebook reminders that turn them into repeat work.

02

Why pressure washing businesses need a lead generation system

  • The owner is usually the crew. In a small operation, the person who should answer the phone is holding a surface cleaner. An automated text plus an AI appointment setter keeps leads from going cold while you work.
  • Homeowners decide fast and price-shop. A house wash or driveway clean is a quick decision, so the first credible quote often wins. Our speed-to-lead automation has a 38-second median first contact across 60+ deployments.
  • Demand is seasonal. Spring cleanups, pre-sale home prep and pre-holiday washes create peaks. A funnel lets you turn ads up and down with the calendar instead of waiting on referrals.
  • Repeat work is where the margin is. Siding, driveways and decks get dirty again. Without a CRM, next year's job goes to whoever knocks on the door first.
03

What we do for pressure washing companies

  1. Visual ads. Split-screen before-and-after clips of your own jobs, filmed on a phone, with the service area named in the copy.
  2. An instant-quote quiz. Surface (house siding, driveway, roof soft wash, deck, fence, commercial), rough size, zip code, timing, optional photos, then a verified mobile number.
  3. A price range up front. Where your pricing follows surface and size, the thank-you page and first text give a range, which keeps price shoppers engaged instead of ghosting.
  4. Booking automation. Simple jobs self-book a slot; roofs, large commercial jobs and anything unusual route to an on-site estimate calendar.
  5. Reviews and rebooking. After the job, a review request, then a reminder months later to book again. Our SMS follow-up templates include the wording.
04

Opportunities pressure washing businesses should target

  • Bundles. House wash plus driveway plus walkways raises the ticket without a second trip.
  • Roof soft washing. A higher-ticket service where homeowners care about method and safety, so a short explainer video earns trust.
  • Commercial and HOA work. Storefronts, parking areas, apartment complexes and dumpster pads bring recurring contracts. These need their own landing page and a call rather than a quiz.
  • Maintenance plans. Pre-sold yearly or twice-yearly cleanings smooth out the slow months.
  • Local search terms. "Pressure washing near me", "house washing" plus your city, "driveway cleaning cost" and "roof soft wash" each deserve a matching page and ad group.
05

Results

We have one documented pressure washing case. For a pressure washing company in Texas, the funnel generated 142 leads at $11.24 per lead on $1,596 of ad spend. That is the only pressure washing figure we publish.

The closest proof comes from other home services campaigns that use the same quote-and-book structure: 337 appointments on $1,621 of spend, 49 leads at $13.03 on $638, and 180 leads at $14.25 on $2,665.70. Roofing estimator funnels, the nearest cousin, have run from $4.27 to $24 per lead. Those are different trades, so read them as a range of what the system does, not a promise for your market.

06

Mistakes pressure washing businesses make

  • Competing only on the lowest price. Rock-bottom driveway offers attract the cheapest buyers. Sell the result and the convenience, and use bundles to protect margin.
  • No service-area filter. Ask for the zip code early so out-of-area leads never reach your calendar.
  • Stock photos. They look like every competitor. Real before-and-after footage from your jobs is the ad.
  • Forgetting the rebook. One reminder text next season is the cheapest lead you will ever get.

Frequently asked questions.

How much do pressure washing leads cost?

Our one documented pressure washing campaign produced 142 leads at $11.24 each on $1,596 of spend in Texas. Related home services campaigns have landed between about $13 and $24 per lead. Your cost depends on market, season, offer and how strictly the funnel filters, so judge it by cost per booked job.

Should I use Facebook ads or Google for pressure washing?

Both, for different jobs. Facebook and Instagram ads create demand with visual before-and-after footage and suit residential house and driveway washing. Google search captures people already looking, which suits urgent and commercial work. Many owners start on Meta because the visuals sell the service, then add search for high-intent terms.

Can I show prices in the funnel?

Often, yes. If your pricing follows square footage or surface type, the funnel can show a range based on the answers, which keeps shoppers engaged. Final prices still depend on condition and access, so we frame it as an estimate and book an on-site check for roofs, large commercial jobs or anything unusual.

How do I get repeat pressure washing customers?

Tag every customer in the CRM by service and date, send a review request after the job, and schedule a rebook reminder for the following season. Offer a maintenance plan on the thank-you page or at the end of the job. Repeat customers cost almost nothing to win back and carry the best margin.

Do you work with pressure washing companies outside Texas?

Yes. Funnels That Print is based in Dubai and works remotely with clients across the US and other countries. Our pressure washing case happens to be a Texas company, but the funnel, follow-up and reminders do not depend on location. We adapt service area, seasonality and offers to your market.

Who answers my leads while I am on a job?

An automated text goes out within seconds. If you do not pick up the call alert, an AI appointment setter calls the homeowner, confirms surface and address, and books a slot or estimate. You review everything in the CRM between jobs. AI calling typically works out to about $3 to $9 per booked appointment.
