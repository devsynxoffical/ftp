Facebook ads for fashion brands that sell the collection, not one viral item

Quick answer

What works for Facebook ads in fashion?

A mix of styled creative and catalog ads. Test try-on and outfit videos, creator content and fabric close-ups, run dynamic catalog ads across the range, and launch new collections as drops with an email and SMS waitlist. Track purchases and returns, and judge campaigns on margin after returns, not ROAS alone.

01

Why fashion brands need Facebook ads with a system behind them

  • Trends move faster than one shoot a season. A creative pipeline that ships weekly keeps pace with what shoppers are wearing and saving.
  • The range is wide. With dozens of styles, sizes and colors, catalog ads let Meta match products to shoppers instead of you guessing which item to push.
  • Returns change the math. Fit problems turn a strong ROAS into a weak margin. Fit guidance before checkout protects it.
  • Launches create spikes. Drops and limited runs produce urgency evergreen ads cannot, as long as the audience is warmed up before launch day.
02

What we do for fashion brands

  1. Creative testing. Try-on hauls, outfit builds, creator content, fabric close-ups and how-to-style videos, tested in weekly batches.
  2. Catalog and collection campaigns. A clean product feed with accurate sizes and availability, dynamic ads for retargeting, and broad prospecting.
  3. Fit-confidence pages. Landing pages for hero products and bundles with fit notes, model height and size worn, measurements and exchange messaging, built by our funnel team.
  4. Drop launches. Teaser and waitlist ads, early access for subscribers, then a short burst of new-arrival ads.
  5. Tracking and review. Conversions API with purchase values, and a weekly review in a funnel KPI dashboard that shows margin after returns.

The weekly review is where fashion accounts are won or lost. We look at spend, purchases and average order value by campaign, then line them up against returns by product so a style that sells well but comes back often gets less budget. Sizes and colors that run out are paused in the feed before ads keep paying to send shoppers to sold-out pages. New creative is briefed from what the data shows, such as which fits, colors and styling angles bought, not from what the team likes best.

03

Opportunities fashion brands should target

  • Outfit bundles. Complete-the-look sets raise order value and remove the guesswork of what goes with what.
  • Seasonal capsules. Small seasonal collections give a fresh reason to advertise without a full range launch.
  • Creator partnerships. Creator try-ons run as partnership ads often feel native in feeds and borrow trust.
  • Back-in-stock moments. Bestsellers that sell out are easy email and SMS revenue when they return.
  • Search language as hooks. Phrases like "linen pants for women", "oversized hoodie" and an occasion plus "dress" mirror how shoppers search and make strong ad headlines.

05

Mistakes fashion brands make

  • Judging ROAS without returns. A campaign at 4x that drives high-return orders can lose money. Track returns by product and campaign.
  • Only lookbook images. Flat studio shots rarely stop the scroll. Movement, fit and real bodies do.
  • Discounting every launch. It trains customers to wait for the sale and erodes full-price demand.
  • A broken product feed. Missing sizes, wrong availability and poor images quietly sink catalog performance.
  • Depending on one viral item. When it fatigues, revenue falls with it. Build creative across the range.

Frequently asked questions.

What ROAS do fashion brands get from Facebook ads?

Our documented apparel cases range from 3.56x to 5.49x ROAS, including $165,000 at 4.6x and $148,000 at 4.7x. Fashion margins and return rates vary widely, so 4x can be great for one brand and break-even for another. Calculate your target with returns included before you scale.

Should fashion brands use catalog ads or creative ads?

Both. Creative ads with try-ons, styling and creator content introduce the brand and win cold shoppers. Catalog ads show the right product to people who browsed or resemble past buyers. Most fashion accounts we run use creative-led prospecting with catalog retargeting, adjusting the split as the range grows.

How do you reduce returns from ad traffic?

Give shoppers what they need to choose the right size before checkout: fit notes, model height and size worn, measurements, fabric details and reviews that mention fit. Video try-ons help more than flat photos. Track returns by product and campaign, and cut ads that drive high-return orders even when their ROAS looks good.

How should we launch a new collection?

Warm up first. Run teaser creative and waitlist ads a week or two before the drop, give subscribers early access, then launch with a short burst of new-arrival ads and catalog campaigns. Limited quantities create real urgency. Follow up with restock or last-chance messages rather than an immediate discount.

Do fashion brands need landing pages or just Shopify?

Shopify product and collection pages work well for retargeting and catalog ads. For cold traffic on a hero product or bundle, a dedicated landing page that matches the ad, with fit guidance and social proof, often converts better. We build these alongside your store rather than replacing it.

How fast can you start on a fashion account?

Landing page and funnel builds go live in 5 to 10 business days. Creative testing can start sooner if tracking is already sound. If it is not, we fix the Conversions API setup first, because scaling on incomplete purchase data wastes budget. Everything starts with an account review on a call.
