AI receptionist

An AI receptionist that answers every call, day and night

An AI receptionist picks up your business line when your team cannot: after hours, at lunch, during a busy morning or while the owner is on a job. It greets callers, finds out what they need, books appointments from your live calendar, transfers urgent calls to the right person and saves a summary of every call to your CRM. Unlike our outbound agents, it is built for people calling you, whether they are new prospects, existing customers or someone with an urgent problem.

Quick answer

What does an AI receptionist do?

It answers inbound calls 24/7, identifies the reason for the call, answers common questions from an approved script, books appointments into your calendar, transfers urgent or high-value calls to staff, and texts back anyone whose call was missed. Every call gets a written summary, tags and next steps in your CRM, so nothing depends on memory.

01

What the AI receptionist handles

  • Answering: picks up on the first rings, around the clock, with your business name and an AI disclosure.
  • Missed-call text-back: if a call still drops, the caller gets a text within seconds asking how you can help.
  • After-hours booking: offers open slots from your calendar so night and weekend callers leave with an appointment.
  • Routing and transfer: emergencies, existing clients or hot prospects are transferred to the right staff member by rules you set.
  • FAQs: hours, service area, what to bring, how estimates work, all from an approved list.
  • Call summaries: reason for the call, details collected and outcome are written to the contact in the CRM.

The real cost of a missed call is rarely visible. Callers who reach voicemail often hang up and dial the next company on the list, and nobody on your team ever knows they called. The AI receptionist turns those silent losses into logged contacts with a reason, a number and usually a booked time, which also tells you how many calls you were missing before. A human receptionist is still better for complex, emotional or high-trust calls. That is why transfer rules are part of every build, and why many clients use the AI only after hours and as overflow.

03

Industry setups

  • Home services, HVAC and roofing: separates emergencies from routine jobs, collects address and issue, books estimate windows and transfers true emergencies to the on-call tech.
  • Clinics and med spas: books consultations, answers prep and policy questions, and transfers anything clinical to staff. The dental version is on our AI voice agents for dental clinics page.
  • Law firms: runs first-pass personal injury intake questions, flags likely cases and transfers to an intake specialist, without giving legal advice.
  • Real estate: answers listing calls, captures buyer or seller details and books showings or valuation calls.

Detailed setups by trade: AI receptionist for HVAC, for plumbers, for contractors and for real estate.

04

How the call flows into your CRM

Inbound calls ring your existing number first if you want them to. Unanswered or after-hours calls forward to the AI receptionist, which runs the conversation, then writes a summary, tags and the booked slot to the contact in GoHighLevel. A new contact enters your pipeline at the right stage and your CRM automation takes over with confirmations and reminders from our no-show reminder sequence. Check what missed calls and no-shows cost you with the no-show cost calculator.

Cost and setup

The build is part of our $1,999 complete suite, which includes the CRM, automation, AI booking agent and tracking. Setup takes 5 to 10 business days: call flow and script, FAQ list, calendar and transfer rules, then at least 50 test calls before your real line is forwarded. Frequently asked questions.

Will callers know they are talking to an AI receptionist?

Yes. The receptionist introduces itself as an AI assistant for your business in its greeting. We have found callers care far more about getting a fast, useful answer and a booked time than about who picks up, and anyone who asks for a person is transferred or offered a callback right away.

Can it transfer calls to my team?

Yes. You set the rules: emergencies to the on-call phone, existing clients to the office, high-value inquiries to the owner, and so on. If nobody answers the transfer, the receptionist takes a message, books a callback and texts the caller, so the call is never simply dropped.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Most clients keep their existing number and forward unanswered or after-hours calls to the AI receptionist. You can also give it a dedicated number for ads or tracking. Forwarding rules can change by time of day, so your staff still take calls during business hours.

Can it take payments or give medical or legal advice?

No. We do not set receptionists up to take card details by voice, diagnose, recommend treatments or give legal opinions. For clinics and law firms the agent collects basic details, books a consultation or intake call and hands anything clinical or legal to a qualified person.

What happens if the AI receptionist does not understand a caller?

It asks one clarifying question, and if it still cannot help, it takes the caller's name, number and reason, promises a callback and creates a task for your team. Strong accents, bad lines and unusual requests are part of our test calls, and we review real call summaries weekly during the first month after launch.

What results can I expect from an AI receptionist?

We do not publish receptionist-only benchmarks yet. Our proof comes from the appointment-booking systems the receptionist plugs into, such as 5,628 HVAC appointments and 337 home services appointments booked for clients. Your result depends on call volume, how many calls you miss today and how fast your team follows up.
