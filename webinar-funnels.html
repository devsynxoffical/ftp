Quick answer

What is a webinar funnel?

A webinar funnel is a sequence that turns ad traffic into webinar registrants, attendees and buyers: a registration page, confirmation and reminder messages, the webinar itself with an offer, then replay and follow-up emails, texts and calls. Its key numbers are cost per registration, show-up rate and sales per attendee.

Free or paid registration?

Free webinars get more registrants; paid webinars get better ones. One client charged $4 per seat and filled 835 paid registrations at $4.57 each, which made the webinar profitable before the pitch because every registrant had already paid. Free events work when the follow-up is strong enough to handle lower intent.

02

The parts we build

  1. Registration page with one promise, the date and a short form.
  2. Confirmation and reminders by email and SMS: immediately, 24 hours, 1 hour and 5 minutes before.
  3. The event: live, automated or hybrid, with the offer and a booking or checkout link.
  4. Replay and follow-up for no-shows and non-buyers, with a deadline.
  5. Sales calls booked from the webinar, protected by reminders.
03

The numbers to watch

Cost per registration, show-up rate, offer conversion and revenue per registrant. One online summit reached 15,958 registrations at $7.48; a webinar for a coaching offer produced $64,960 from $2,184 in ad spend. Model your own with the ROAS calculator.

04

Who uses webinar funnels

Coaches, course creators, consultants and B2B software companies that need time to teach before they sell. For shorter explanations, a VSL funnel is usually more efficient; for low-priced offers, see the low-ticket funnel.

Frequently asked questions.

Live or automated webinar?

Start live: you learn which parts of the presentation land, handle real questions and close more. Once the presentation converts consistently, an automated or hybrid version scales it without you presenting every time. Many clients run live monthly and automated in between.

How do you increase webinar show-up rates?

Short time between registration and the event, a confirmation that asks registrants to add it to their calendar, reminders by email and SMS at 24 hours, 1 hour and 5 minutes, and a reason to attend live, such as a bonus or live Q&A. Paid registration also lifts attendance.

What should a webinar registration page include?

One clear promise, the date and time in the visitor's time zone, who it is for, what they will learn in three bullets, a short form and proof of the presenter. Everything else lowers conversion. Keep it short enough to read without scrolling on a phone.

What is a good cost per webinar registration?

Our campaigns range from about $4.57 for paid registrations to $7.48 for a large online summit. It depends heavily on niche and offer. The more useful number is revenue per registrant, which tells you how much you can pay for each registration.

Do webinars work for high-ticket offers?

Yes. The webinar teaches and builds trust, then invites qualified attendees to book a call. Several of our coaching results came from webinars selling programs priced in the thousands, with the sale closed on a call booked during or after the event.
