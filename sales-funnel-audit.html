Quick answer

What is a sales funnel audit?

A sales funnel audit is a step-by-step review of how traffic becomes revenue, measuring the drop-off at each stage from ad click to closed deal. It finds the biggest leaks, such as slow follow-up, weak pages or broken tracking, and ranks fixes by revenue impact so you repair the costliest problem first.

02

What we usually find

Most owners expect the problem to be the ads or the page. In our audits, the larger loss usually sits after the form:

  • Slow first contact. Leads wait hours for a call. Across 60+ deployments of our speed-to-lead automation, median first contact fell to 38 seconds, 84% of leads were reached inside the first minute, and lead-to-booking rose 2.4x on the same ads.
  • Missing reminders. One reminder email, or none. With three-channel reminders by text, email and call, show rates reach up to 88%.
  • Leads never worked. Contacts sit in the CRM with no owner, no stage and no second attempt.
  • Tracking that undercounts. Pixel-only setups that miss events, so Meta optimizes for form fills instead of buyers.

To see what no-shows cost you today, run the no-show cost calculator before the audit.

03

The order we fix leaks in

We fix from the bottom of the funnel up, because a leak late in the funnel wastes everything spent above it:

  1. Follow-up speed and attempts, often with an AI appointment setter that calls and texts new leads within seconds.
  2. Reminders and show rate, using a sequence like our no-show reminder sequence.
  3. Tracking, so the ad platform learns from booked and closed deals.
  4. Form and page: friction, speed, mobile layout and message match.
  5. Offer and ads, once everything below them is measured correctly.

Fixing the bottom first also makes later tests honest: you cannot judge a new ad while half its leads go uncalled.

04

What you receive

  • A stage-by-stage scorecard with conversion rates from click to closed deal, built on our funnel KPI dashboard template.
  • A ranked leak list with the estimated monthly revenue each leak costs.
  • A recorded walkthrough of the funnel as a lead experiences it, including how long it took to hear back.
  • A fix plan that separates what your team can change this week from what needs a rebuild.
05

How we run the audit

We start by acting as a lead: we click a live ad, fill in the form on a phone, and time how long it takes to hear back and what the follow-up says. Then we pull the numbers for each stage from the ad account, CRM and calendar for the last 30 to 90 days, and compare what the platforms report with what your team actually booked and closed. Gaps between those numbers are often the first finding. Finally we rank every leak by the revenue it costs per month, so the fix plan starts with the change that pays back fastest.

06

Pricing and when to audit

The audit is scoped on a call because the work depends on how many funnels, ad accounts and CRMs are involved. When you go ahead with a rebuild, the audit is included as its first step. An audit makes sense when cost per lead looks fine but sales do not follow, when show rates are falling, or when you are about to raise ad spend. Book a call to scope yours.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a sales funnel audit cost?

We scope each audit on a call, because the effort depends on how many funnels, ad accounts and CRM pipelines are involved. We do not publish a fixed audit price. If you go ahead with a funnel rebuild with us, the audit is included as the first step rather than charged separately.

How long does an audit take?

We agree the timeline on the strategy call, based on how many funnels, ad accounts and automations are involved, and start once we have read access to your ad account, pages, CRM and tracking. We also submit a test lead to experience the follow-up firsthand. You then receive the scorecard, ranked leak list, recorded walkthrough and fix plan, and we go through them together on a call.

What access do you need?

Read access to your Meta or Google ad account, the funnel builder or website, the CRM and calendar, and any analytics or tag manager. Nothing is changed during the audit. If you already track results in a spreadsheet, share it so we can compare it against what the platforms report.

What is the most common problem you find?

Slow or missing follow-up after the form. Many funnels generate enough leads but call them hours later, try once and send no reminders. Fixing that usually lifts booked calls faster than any new ad or page, which is why we fix the bottom of the funnel first.

Can my team implement the fixes ourselves?

Yes. The fix plan is written so an in-house marketer or VA can handle the simpler items, such as shortening a form or adding reminders. Larger items like rebuilding a page, setting up the Conversions API or adding an AI appointment setter can be done by us as a separate project.

Do you audit eCommerce funnels too?

Yes. For stores we review product and landing pages, offers and bundles, checkout friction, order bumps and upsells, abandoned cart flows and purchase tracking with value. The leaks differ from lead generation, but the method of measuring every step and fixing the costliest first is the same.
