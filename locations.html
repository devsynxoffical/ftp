01

How we work with US clients from Dubai

Lead generation is not a door-to-door business. The work happens in ad accounts, funnel pages, a CRM and a phone system, all of which run in the cloud. We build and manage those from Dubai, and you keep ownership of every account.

  • Kickoff on video. One call to map your offer, service area, ticket size and who picks up leads. You can book a call at a time that suits US hours.
  • Build in 5 to 10 business days. Funnel, CRM automation, consent language and tracking go live together.
  • Follow-up that never sleeps. Our speed-to-lead automation contacts new leads in seconds in your time zone, and an AI appointment setter covers nights and weekends.
  • Weekly numbers. Cost per lead, cost per booked appointment and show rate, reported in writing.

The time difference helps more than it hurts: changes we ship during the Dubai day are live before most US teams start work.

Frequently asked questions.

Why do you have state pages if you have no US offices?

Because people search for help in their state, and the honest answer is useful: we have no local office, but we have run real campaigns for businesses there. Each state page lists only the cases we can tie to that state, so you can judge the evidence yourself before booking a call.

Which US states have you worked in the most?

Texas, by a wide margin. Our case files include Texas roofing, personal injury, mortgage, real estate, solar, remodeling and pressure washing campaigns. Florida and New York come next, with HVAC, legal, home services and med spa work. California has two documented campaigns, roofing and med spa.

Do you work with businesses in states not listed here?

Yes. The funnel and follow-up system does not depend on location, and we have clients across the US as well as in Canada, the UK and Australia. We only create a state page where we have documented results there, so a missing state means missing evidence, not a refusal to work there.

How do time zones work with a Dubai agency?

Your leads are contacted by automation and AI agents in your local time, so nothing waits for us. Calls with our team are scheduled in US morning or evening hours. Because Dubai is ahead, most fixes and new ad variations are live before your business day starts.

Is pricing different by state?

No. Every build is a one-time $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend and software are paid directly by you, and costs per lead vary by market.
