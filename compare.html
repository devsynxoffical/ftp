Quick answer

Which funnel platform or service should I choose?

Pick by where your funnel ends. Booked-call offers usually belong in GoHighLevel. Larger sales teams with long cycles often fit HubSpot. Course and product sellers lean toward ClickFunnels, Kajabi or Systeme.io. For follow-up, AI voice agents win on speed and cost, while human setters still win on complex conversations.

How we write these comparisons

Every comparison follows the same rules. We lead with a verdict, then show a table covering pricing, features, best fit and limitations. Third-party prices are list prices as of November 2026, checked against current published pricing; vendors change them often, so confirm on their pricing page before you buy. Where we could not confirm a number, we describe the tier instead of guessing.

We also say how we use each option for clients. Most of our lead generation builds run on GoHighLevel, and most product funnels use a ClickFunnels checkout, but we integrate HubSpot, Kajabi and Shopify when a business already depends on them. Moving platforms is rarely free, so a good reason to stay is a valid answer.

If you are still deciding what the funnel itself should look like, start with our services overview or model the numbers with the funnel conversion calculator.

04

The question to answer before any comparison

Software rarely decides results on its own. What decides cost per booked call or cost per sale is the offer, the front end, how fast leads get a response and whether the ad platform learns who buys. A $97 tool with 60-second follow-up beats an $890 tool where leads wait a day. So before picking a platform, write down where your funnel ends (a call, a checkout, an application or a course login), how many people will work the leads, and what you already pay for. Those three answers settle most of these comparisons in a few minutes.

Frequently asked questions.

Are these comparisons sponsored?

No. Nothing on these pages is paid placement, and we do not rank tools by commission. We build on whichever platform fits the client's offer and existing stack, and every comparison says where the competing option is the better choice, including cases where we would recommend a tool we do not use ourselves.

How current are the prices?

Third-party prices are list prices as of November 2026, taken from current published pricing. Vendors change tiers, add usage fees and run promotions often, so treat the figures as a starting point and confirm on the vendor's own pricing page. Our own build prices are one-time fees of $999, $1,499 or $1,999.

Which platform do you use most?

GoHighLevel, for most lead generation builds that end in a booked call, because the CRM, SMS, email, calendar and automation live in one account. ClickFunnels is our usual pick for product checkouts with order bumps and upsells, and we connect it to GoHighLevel for follow-up. We integrate HubSpot when a sales team already runs on it.

Can you help me migrate from one platform to another?

Yes. A migration usually covers contacts and tags, pipelines, forms, calendars, email and SMS sequences, domains and tracking. We rebuild the funnel and automations, test every path, then switch traffic over. Most migrations fit inside the same 5 to 10 business day window as a new build when the scope is a single funnel.

What if I am comparing agencies rather than software?

Read the funnel agency vs freelancer comparison. It covers price ranges, who owns the outcome, how long builds take and when a single freelancer is the smarter hire. The short version: one page from a specialist is cheaper, but a system of copy, build, automation and tracking is easier to get right with one accountable team.
