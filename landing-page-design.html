Quick answer

What does a landing page design agency do?

It designs and builds single-purpose pages that turn ad clicks into a specific action: a form, a booked call or a purchase. That includes the layout, copy, mobile experience, page speed, form or checkout, and tracking. The goal is a higher conversion rate from the same traffic, not just a better-looking page.

02

What makes our landing pages convert

  1. Message match. The headline repeats the promise from the ad, so the visitor knows they are in the right place.
  2. Mobile first. Most paid social traffic arrives on a phone, so we design the phone view first and the desktop view second.
  3. Speed. Light images, few scripts and no heavy page-builder bloat. Every extra second costs visitors.
  4. Copy before design. We write the page, then design around the words. The structure we use is in our free sales funnel copy template.
  5. Low form friction. Only the fields you need, with optional OTP phone verification so fake numbers never reach the CRM.
  6. Proof near the ask. Reviews, results and guarantees placed next to the button, not buried at the bottom.
03

Tracking and follow-up are part of the page

A page that converts but is not tracked cannot be improved, and a lead nobody calls is wasted. Every page we build includes the Meta pixel and Conversions API events, Google tags where needed, UTM capture into the CRM, and a thank-you page that fires the conversion once. Leads go straight into a pipeline with instant text and email follow-up, or into an AI appointment setter on the $1,999 suite.

04

GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels or custom code

  • GoHighLevel for lead generation, because the page, CRM, calendar and automations share one account. See our GoHighLevel agency work.
  • ClickFunnels for product sales with order bumps and one-click upsells, the structure behind our $847K low-ticket funnel.
  • Custom code when speed, design control or an existing website stack matters more than drag-and-drop editing.

We recommend the platform after hearing your offer. The trade-offs are covered in GoHighLevel vs ClickFunnels.

After launch we review conversion data and rewrite the weakest section first. If you already have pages that underperform, start with a sales funnel audit instead.

06

Common landing page mistakes we fix

  • Several offers on one page. A landing page should ask for one action; menus and extra links send visitors away.
  • A homepage used as a landing page. Paid traffic sent to a general site has to hunt for the next step.
  • Generic headlines. "Quality service you can trust" says nothing; a specific outcome, timeframe or price does.
  • Thank-you pages left blank. This is where you confirm the call, explain what happens next and cut no-shows.
  • Testing too much at once. We change one element per test so the result tells you why it worked.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does landing page design cost?

Our packages are one-time fees: $999 for Design Only, where you supply the copy, and $1,499 for Design + Copy Build, where we write it too. The $1,999 suite adds CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. There is no monthly retainer for the build itself.

How long does it take to build a landing page?

Most landing pages go live in 5 to 10 business days from kickoff. That covers copy, wireframe, design, build, form and calendar connection, tracking and testing on mobile and desktop. Quiz pages and multi-page funnels with automations sit at the longer end of that range.

Do you write the copy?

Yes, on the Design + Copy Build and complete suite packages. We write the headline, body, calls to action, form questions and thank-you page for your specific buyer and offer. On Design Only you provide the copy and we design around it, with suggestions where the structure needs work.

Which platform will my page be built on?

Usually GoHighLevel for lead generation, ClickFunnels for product sales with order bumps and upsells, or custom code when speed or design control matters most. The page is built in your own account, so you own it and can edit it after handover.

Can you redesign an existing landing page?

Yes. We first check where the page loses visitors: message match with the ad, load time, mobile layout, form length and proof. Sometimes the page is fine and the leak is after the form, in follow-up or reminders, and we will tell you that rather than sell you a redesign.

Will the page be tracked for Meta and Google ads?

Yes. We install the Meta pixel with Conversions API events, Google tags where you run Google Ads, and pass UTM parameters into the CRM. Conversions fire once on the thank-you page or booking, so ad platforms optimize on real leads and you can see which ads produce customers.
