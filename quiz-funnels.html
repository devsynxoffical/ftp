Quick answer

What is a quiz funnel?

A quiz funnel is a lead funnel that asks four to six questions, one per screen, before collecting contact details. Each small answer builds commitment, so more visitors finish, and the answers qualify and segment the lead. Qualified leads go to a booking calendar or offer; others go to a lower-priced path or nurture.

Why quizzes beat forms on cold traffic

Across 275+ split-tested funnels, four-to-six step quiz front ends completed at 64.8% against an 18% view-through rate for VSL-first pages, and cut cost per lead by 34%. The reason is psychology: small, easy answers first, contact details last. Full data in VSL vs quiz funnel.

How to write quiz questions that convert

  • Lead with curiosity, not qualification. The first question should be easy and interesting.
  • One question per screen with a visible progress bar.
  • Budget and timeline third or fourth, once momentum exists.
  • Use the answers in the results page and follow-up copy.

Routing and follow-up

The answers decide what happens next: qualified leads see a short bridge video and the calendar, unqualified leads get a lower-cost offer or a resource, and every lead gets speed-to-lead follow-up. Pair a quiz with a VSL for high-ticket offers. Check the economics in the cost per lead calculator.

Quiz funnels

Frequently asked questions.

How many questions should a quiz funnel have?

Four to six. Fewer than four does not build enough commitment or collect enough qualifying data; more than seven starts to feel like an application and completion falls. Put the easiest, most engaging question first and the contact details last.

Do quiz funnels lower lead quality?

Not when the questions do real qualification work. Asking about budget, timeline and situation lets you route unqualified respondents to a lower-cost path instead of the sales calendar, which usually raises the quality of booked calls rather than lowering it.

Which platform do you build quizzes on?

GoHighLevel's multi-step forms and surveys handle most quiz funnels and connect directly to the CRM. For high-traffic or design-heavy quizzes we build custom server-rendered pages with client-side steps, so there is no page load between questions. Either way, every answer is saved to the contact record and can trigger different follow-up.

Can a quiz funnel work for eCommerce?

Yes. Product finder quizzes recommend the right product, bundle or routine based on the answers, which lifts conversion and average order value, especially in skincare, supplements and apparel where buyers are unsure what fits them. The answers also segment email and SMS follow-up by need.

How do I track where people drop off?

We fire an event at each step, so you can see the exact question where visitors abandon. The weakest step gets rewritten or removed first, and the final submission is sent to the ad platform as the conversion event. Qualified completions can be sent as a separate event so ads optimize for fit, not volume.

How much does a quiz funnel cost?

Quiz funnels are built inside our standard packages: $1,499 for design, copy and a full build, or $1,999 with CRM automation, an AI booking agent and Conversions API tracking. Ad spend and software subscriptions are paid directly by you. Most quizzes launch within 5 to 10 business days.
