Roofing Lead Generation Case Studies

Roofing is one of the most expensive lead markets in home services. Replacement jobs are worth $10,000 to $40,000, so every roofer bids on the same homeowners and lead marketplaces sell each request to three to five companies at once.

These case studies show the alternative: exclusive roofing leads from a funnel the roofer owns. Each one documents the cost per lead, lead volume, ad spend and, where available, booked inspections and closed jobs, taken from the client's ad account and CRM.

Quick answer

What cost per lead do roofing lead generation funnels achieve?

Across the documented roofing campaigns, cost per lead ranges from $4.27 to about $24. Examples include 537 leads at $4.27 on $2,292 of spend, 686 leads at $5.31, 1,068 leads at $17.40 and 1,024 leads at $24 on $24,902. One campaign booked 52 calls on $646 of ad spend. Market, season and offer drive the spread.

How we build roofing funnels

  1. Quote estimator front end

    A 4-step estimator (roof type, issue, timeline, address) replaces the contact form and converts two to three times better on cold traffic.

  2. Storm, age and financing ad angles

    Geo-targeted Meta campaigns around hail events, roof age and monthly-payment financing, with local crew footage instead of stock photos.

  3. 60-second follow-up

    Instant SMS, an immediate call from a rep or AI setter, and missed-call text-back so the first roofer to call is you.

  4. Tracking jobs, not form fills

    Inspection booked and contract signed are sent back to Meta so the algorithm finds homeowners who actually buy.

About roofing results

Frequently asked questions.

What cost per lead do these roofing campaigns achieve?

It varies by market and season. The documented roofing cases run from $4.27 per lead at the low end to about $24 in competitive markets, with several between $5 and $6. Each case on this page shows its actual cost per lead, lead volume and ad spend rather than a blended average, so you can compare like with like.

Are these leads shared with other roofers?

No. Every lead comes from a funnel and ad account owned by the roofing company, so the homeowner only speaks to that company. That is the main difference from lead marketplaces, which often sell the same request to three to five contractors and leave everyone racing to call first and compete on price.

How fast can a roofing funnel be built?

The estimator page, Meta ads setup, follow-up automation and conversion tracking are built and live in 5 to 10 business days. Most of that time goes into the quote estimator, local ad angles and the 60-second follow-up sequence, so the system is ready to take leads the day campaigns launch.

How much does a roofing funnel build cost?

Builds are a one-time fee rather than a retainer: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Meta from the roofer's own account.

Who owns the funnel, ad account and leads?

The roofing company does. The funnel, CRM, automations, ad account and every lead in the pipeline belong to the business, not to us. If you stop working with us, everything keeps running and the data stays yours, which is why results can compound over time instead of resetting with each new vendor.

What happens after a homeowner submits the estimator?

They get an instant SMS, an immediate call from a rep or AI setter, and missed-call text-back if nobody answers. Across 60+ deployments of this follow-up, median first contact is 38 seconds and lead-to-booking improves 2.4x, so inspections get booked while the homeowner is still engaged and before competitors call back.
