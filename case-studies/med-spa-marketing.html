Med Spa Marketing Case Studies

Med spas and aesthetic clinics compete on trust and convenience. The patient is choosing who will treat their face or body, and usually books with whoever responds first and looks most credible.

The cases below show lead and booking results for injectables, laser, skin and body treatments, built on treatment-matcher funnels with before-and-after proof and automated booking and reminders.

What results do med spa marketing funnels produce?

Documented med spa campaigns include 1,011 leads on $4,703 of ad spend, $11,700 in revenue from 45 leads on $590 (a 19.8x return), $74,100 from 303 clients on $10K of spend, and $639,000 in revenue from 178 clients. One campaign reached a $0.50 cost per lead. Treatment and offer drive the spread.

How we build med spa funnels

  1. Treatment matcher

    A 3-step quiz on concern, area and timing recommends a treatment and captures the lead with context the front desk can use.

  2. Proof-led creative

    Before-and-after results, provider introductions and offer-led ads tested against each other.

  3. Booking and reminders

    Direct calendar booking with SMS reminders and confirmation to protect consultation show rates.

  4. Review and rebook flows

    Post-treatment review requests and rebooking reminders to lift lifetime value.

About med spa results

Frequently asked questions.

Which med spa treatments do these campaigns cover?

Injectables, laser hair removal, skin treatments and body contouring offers. Each case lists the niche, offer and result, so you can compare campaigns for treatments similar to yours. The funnel structure stays the same across treatments, while the quiz questions, before-and-after proof and booking offer change for each service line.

How do you keep consultation no-shows down?

With a confirmation step at booking and a multi-touch SMS and email reminder sequence tied to the appointment. Three-channel reminders have lifted show-up rates as high as 88% in our deployments. Missed consultations trigger an automatic rebook message, so a no-show becomes a rescheduled visit instead of a lost lead.

Do you build the booking system?

Yes. The funnel, calendar, reminders and follow-up are built and connected to the clinic's CRM. Leads can book directly from the treatment matcher, and the front desk sees each patient's concern, area and timing before the consultation, which makes the first conversation faster and more personal.

How much does a med spa funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and paid from the clinic's own Meta account, which the clinic keeps.

Are med spa ads restricted on Meta?

Yes, health and cosmetic procedure ads face stricter review. Creative must avoid negative body image, unrealistic promises and some before-and-after formats, and claims must match what providers can support. We write within those limits, and the clinic's medical director reviews treatment claims before campaigns go live.

How quickly can a med spa start booking consultations?

The treatment matcher, booking calendar, reminders and tracking are built in 5 to 10 business days. Consultations can start booking as soon as ads launch, and early weeks are used to test offers and creative. Booked and attended appointments are tracked so budget shifts toward the treatments that fill the calendar.
