About agency results

Frequently asked questions.

Do these cases include agencies getting results for their clients?

Yes. Several cases show agencies we worked with generating leads and revenue for their own customers using the same system. Others show agencies landing clients for themselves, such as one that closed $15,900 in 10 days. Both use the same offer testing, follow-up and pipeline reporting described on this page.

Does this work for SaaS?

Yes, for SaaS with a sales-assisted motion or a high enough contract value to support paid acquisition. The SaaS cases show leads, demos and sign-ups. Self-serve products with very low monthly pricing usually do better with free trials and product-led growth than with a funnel built around booked demo calls.

Which CRM do you use for B2B?

Most builds run on GoHighLevel, which covers funnels, pipelines, SMS, email and calendars in one place. We also integrate with HubSpot and other CRMs when a sales team already lives there, syncing leads, call outcomes and deal stages so reporting stays accurate from first click to closed deal.

How much does a B2B or agency funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and runs through your own account, with no retainer or revenue share.

How long does a build take?

The offer page, lead magnet or calculator, booking flow, follow-up automation and pipeline reporting are live in 5 to 10 business days. B2B sales cycles are longer than consumer ones, so expect to judge results on booked calls and proposals in the first weeks and on closed revenue over the following months.

Can an AI agent qualify B2B leads?

Yes. An AI voice agent can call new leads within seconds, ask qualifying questions and book demos or strategy calls. AI calling typically costs about $0.10 to $0.25 per minute, roughly $3 to $9 per booked appointment, compared with $40 to $120 when human setters do the same work.
