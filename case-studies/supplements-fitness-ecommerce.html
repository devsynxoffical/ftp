About supplement results

Frequently asked questions.

How do you keep supplement ads compliant?

Creative avoids medical and prohibited claims and focuses on routine, ingredients and customer experience. Ads do not promise to treat, cure or prevent conditions, avoid before-and-after weight-loss imagery and steer clear of language that calls out personal attributes. The brand reviews ingredient claims against its own substantiation before anything goes live.

Do you build subscription funnels?

Yes. Subscribe-and-save and multi-pack offers are part of most builds in this category. The first order often only breaks even, so subscriptions and refills create the profit. Reorder reminders are timed to product usage, and cancellation saves such as skipping or pausing a shipment protect lifetime value.

Does this cover fitness equipment?

Yes. Fitness and health product brands are included alongside supplements and nutrition. Equipment brands rely more on demonstration video and financing or bundle offers than on subscriptions, but the same structure applies: proof-led creative, an education-first landing page, post-purchase upsells and server-side tracking for accurate ROAS.

How much does a supplement funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and runs through your own Meta account, which stays under your control.

How long does a build take?

Advertorials, landing pages, subscription offers, retention flows and server-side tracking are live in 5 to 10 business days. Compliance review of claims is built into that window, so the brand should have ingredient documentation ready at kickoff to avoid delays before creative testing begins on the new funnel.
