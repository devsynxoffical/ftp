Quick answer

What did this medspa campaign achieve?

This medspa campaign produced 1,011 leads. Reported figures: leads 1,011, ad spend $4,703. It ran on paid ads, funnel and follow-up, and the original breakdown was published in March 2026. Its lead volume of 1,011 is the highest of 4 med spa campaigns we have published with this figure, better than the median of 46.

The full breakdown

I won’t reveal the name but if you’re in those areas, chances are their ads have already shown up on your feed.

In the last 28 days:

  • $4,703 in ad spend
  • 1,011 verified patient leads generated
  • 2.01% average CTR

But the real story isn’t the lead volume, it’s the patient conversion system behind it.

  • 3,155 people clicked the ads →
  • 2,902 landed on the page (91.9% retention) →
  • 1,011 completed the consultation form (34.8% landing conversion)

Which means nearly 1 out of every 3 visitors became a pre-qualified patient, not just someone asking for prices, but prospects filtered through our backend qualification flow before ever reaching the clinic.

And this was across two competitive locations, where audiences already see dozens of aesthetic ads daily.

  • No gimmicks.
  • No discount dependency.
  • No vanity metrics.

Just the right treatment positioning, a strong offer, and a funnel engineered to turn curiosity into booked consultations.

Most medspas try to run more ads.

We focus on making every click turn into a serious patient.
