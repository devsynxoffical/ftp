How this low-ticket system is built

  1. Front-end offer

    A low-priced product that solves one specific problem and leads naturally to the backend.

  2. Order bump and upsell

    A one-click bump and post-purchase upsell lift average order value above cost per buyer.

  3. Buyer follow-up

    Email and SMS sequences ascend buyers to the core offer or a sales call.

  4. Value-based tracking

    Purchase value is sent back to Meta so the algorithm optimizes for buyers, not opt-ins.
