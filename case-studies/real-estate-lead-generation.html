Real Estate Lead Generation Case Studies

Real estate leads are cheap to generate and hard to convert. Most buyer and seller leads are months from transacting, so the follow-up system matters more than the ad.

These cases show real estate campaigns with lead volume, cost per lead and spend, combined with qualification and long-term nurture so agents spend time on people ready to move.

What cost per lead do real estate funnels achieve?

Documented real estate campaigns range from $11.57 to $28 per lead. Examples include 69 leads at $11.57 on $798 of spend, 71 leads at $12.12 on $860, 247 leads at $16.48 on $4,069 and 157 leads at $28 on $4,397. Buyer versus seller focus, price band and market explain most of the difference.

About real estate results

Frequently asked questions.

Do these campaigns target buyers or sellers?

Both. Seller valuation funnels and buyer search or pre-approval funnels use different questions, ads and follow-up. Seller leads often cost more but carry a listing, while buyer leads are cheaper and need more nurture. Most agents run one side first, then add the other once the follow-up system is working.

How do you deal with long buying timelines?

Qualifying questions on timeline route leads into immediate follow-up or a long-term nurture sequence. Leads ready in the next 90 days get a call and SMS right away, while later leads receive market updates, new listings and periodic check-ins by text and email until their plans firm up and they raise a hand.

Can teams and brokerages use this?

Yes. Leads can be distributed round-robin across agents with tracking by source, so the team lead sees which campaigns and agents turn leads into appointments and signed agreements. Response-time alerts and reassignment rules make sure no lead sits untouched when an agent is busy showing homes.

How much does a real estate funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and runs through the agent's or brokerage's own Meta account.

Are there advertising rules for real estate ads?

Yes. Meta classifies housing as a special ad category, which limits targeting by age, gender and ZIP code, and fair housing rules prohibit discriminatory messaging. Campaigns are built inside those limits using broad location targeting and creative focused on listings, valuations and market updates rather than on who the buyer is.

How long until an agent gets leads?

The buyer and seller funnels, area-specific ads, follow-up automation and CRM pipeline are live in 5 to 10 business days. Leads start arriving once campaigns launch. Because many leads are months from transacting, judge results on appointments and signed agreements over a few months, not just on first-week lead volume.
