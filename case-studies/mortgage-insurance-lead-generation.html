How we build mortgage funnels

  1. Eligibility calculator front end

    A short calculator on loan type, value, credit band and timeline replaces a plain form and qualifies the borrower before contact.

  2. OTP verification

    Every lead confirms their phone number, which removes fake and mistyped numbers before they reach a loan officer.

  3. Speed-to-lead and nurture

    Instant SMS and a call, then a nurture sequence for borrowers who are not ready yet.

  4. Funded-loan tracking

    Applications and fundings are sent back to Meta so the campaign learns who closes.

About mortgage results

Frequently asked questions.

Do these mortgage campaigns work for refinance and purchase?

Yes. The cases include both purchase and refinance campaigns. The front-end questions and ad angles change with the loan type and rate environment, while the follow-up system stays the same: instant SMS and a call, then a nurture sequence for borrowers who are still shopping or not ready to apply.

How are mortgage leads verified?

Through OTP phone verification on submission and qualifying questions on loan type, property value, credit band and timeline. Borrowers confirm a one-time code before the lead is created, so loan officers stop wasting dials on fake or mistyped numbers and can prioritize applicants who are actually close to applying.

Can this be used for insurance agents?

Yes. The final expense cases on this page use the same funnel structure with insurance-specific qualifying questions such as age range, coverage amount and beneficiary. Verification, speed-to-lead follow-up and appointment reminders work the same way, and agents can book phone or video consultations directly from the funnel.

How do you handle compliance for mortgage and insurance ads?

Meta treats credit and financial-product ads as a special ad category, so targeting is limited and creative must avoid discriminatory or misleading claims. We build campaigns inside those limits, use consent language and opt-in for SMS follow-up, and the lender or agency reviews disclosures and licensing details before anything goes live.

How much does a mortgage funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend runs through the broker's own Meta account, so there is no per-lead charge or revenue share.

How long does it take to launch?

The eligibility calculator, OTP verification, follow-up and funded-loan tracking are live in 5 to 10 business days. Campaigns then spend the first weeks testing angles and audiences, and application and funding events are sent back to Meta so lead quality improves as the account learns which borrowers close.
