How we build coaching funnels

  1. Diagnostic or VSL front end

    A short diagnostic quiz or a focused VSL qualifies the prospect before the calendar.

  2. Application and booking

    Budget, timeline and situation questions route qualified prospects to the closer's calendar.

  3. Show-up sequence

    A multi-channel reminder sequence and pre-call video protect attendance.

  4. Revenue attribution

    Booked, attended and closed calls are sent back to Meta through the Conversions API.

About coaching results

Frequently asked questions.

What kind of coaching offers are these?

Business, agency and service-provider coaching and consulting offers, usually priced from $2,000 to $10,000 or more and sold on a sales call. Lower-priced programs and courses appear too, typically through webinars. Each case lists the offer type, so you can compare funnels built for price points and buyers similar to yours.

Do you build webinar funnels?

Yes. The webinar cases on this page use registration pages, reminder sequences and replay follow-up with booked-call or direct-sale offers. One documented webinar campaign generated 15,958+ registrations at $7.48 per registration, and reminders across email and SMS keep live attendance up so more registrants actually see the offer.

Where is the $1.52M case?

It has its own detailed page at /milliondollarcasestudy covering the VSL funnel, qualification, follow-up and tracking. That page explains how a high-ticket consulting offer collected $1.52M from cold paid traffic, including how reminder automation raised show-up rates and how Conversions API data lowered cost per booked call over time.

How much does a coaching funnel cost to build?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate. There is no revenue share, so a funnel that sells well does not cost you more.

How long does it take to launch a VSL or application funnel?

The VSL or diagnostic front end, application, booking calendar, show-up sequence and revenue tracking are built in 5 to 10 business days. Most of the time goes into the script and qualification questions, because those decide who reaches the closer's calendar and how many booked calls actually show and buy.

Do you provide the sales team or closers?

No. We build the funnel, follow-up, reminders and tracking, and your team or closers run the calls. The $1,999 suite can add an AI booking agent that qualifies and books leads before a human gets involved, and pipeline reporting shows which campaigns produce calls that show and close.
