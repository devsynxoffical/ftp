Quick answer

What did this webinar campaign achieve?

This webinar campaign produced $65K revenue on $2,184 ad spend. Reported figures: revenue $64,960, appointments 140, ad spend $2,184, roas 29.7x. It ran on paid ads, funnel and follow-up, and the original breakdown was published in July 2025. Its return on ad spend of 29.7x is the 2nd highest of 3 coaching and consulting campaigns we have published with this figure, equal to the median of 29.7x.

How this coaching system is built

  1. Diagnostic or VSL front end

    A short diagnostic quiz or a focused VSL qualifies the prospect before the calendar.

  2. Application and booking

    Budget, timeline and situation questions route qualified prospects to the closer's calendar.

  3. Show-up sequence

    A multi-channel reminder sequence and pre-call video protect attendance.

  4. Revenue attribution

    Booked, attended and closed calls are sent back to Meta through the Conversions API.
