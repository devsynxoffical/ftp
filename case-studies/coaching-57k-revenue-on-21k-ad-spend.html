How this coaching system is built

  1. Diagnostic or VSL front end

    A short diagnostic quiz or a focused VSL qualifies the prospect before the calendar.

  2. Application and booking

    Budget, timeline and situation questions route qualified prospects to the closer's calendar.

  3. Show-up sequence

    A multi-channel reminder sequence and pre-call video protect attendance.

  4. Revenue attribution

    Booked, attended and closed calls are sent back to Meta through the Conversions API.
