About fashion results

Frequently asked questions.

Does this work for small fashion brands?

Yes, as long as the margin supports paid acquisition. Offer structure matters more than catalog size: a focused hero product or outfit bundle with clear fit information often scales better than a large catalog. Each case on this page shows revenue and ROAS, so you can compare brands at a similar stage to yours.

What creative works for apparel?

Try-on, styling and fit content from creators, rotated with each collection. Videos that show the garment moving, on different body types and in real settings answer sizing questions before the click, which lifts conversion rate and reduces returns. Static lookbook images still help for retargeting, but video usually carries cold traffic.

How do you plan seasonal campaigns?

Budgets and offers are planned around drops, holidays and Q4, as several cases show. Pre-launch audiences are built with teasers and waitlists, launch day gets the largest budget, and sell-through messaging follows. Holiday campaigns start weeks early so creative is tested before costs rise during Black Friday and December.

How do returns affect ROAS?

Returns can make reported ROAS look better than real profit, especially in apparel. We send purchase events with value through server-side tracking and push fit and sizing information into creative and landing pages so buyers choose correctly. Brands should judge results on net revenue after returns, not only on ad account ROAS.

How much does a fashion funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and runs through your own Meta account, so budget and data stay with you.

How long does a build take?

Landing pages, bundle offers, post-purchase add-ons and server-side tracking are live in 5 to 10 business days. If you are planning a collection drop, start the build a few weeks ahead so pre-launch audiences, creator content and tracking are ready before launch day rather than built under deadline pressure.
