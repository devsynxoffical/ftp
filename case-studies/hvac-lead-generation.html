About HVAC results

Frequently asked questions.

Do these HVAC campaigns target repairs or replacements?

Mostly installs and replacements, where the job value supports paid acquisition. The qualifying questions on system type, age, problem and timeline route repair calls separately, so service techs get repair work and comfort advisors get replacement opportunities. That split keeps cost per sold install measurable instead of blended with low-value calls.

How do you handle seasonality?

Offers and budgets shift between pre-season tune-ups, failure-season replacements and financing angles as demand changes through the year. Spend increases when heatwaves and cold snaps drive urgent requests and eases in shoulder months, when nurture sequences and maintenance offers keep the pipeline warm until the next peak.

What follow-up is used?

Instant SMS and a call, plus missed-call text-back on the inbound line and a nurture sequence for homeowners still comparing quotes. Across 60+ speed-to-lead deployments, median first contact is 38 seconds and 84% of leads are reached inside the first minute, which matters when the homeowner is calling every contractor nearby.

How much does an HVAC funnel cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and paid to Meta from your own ad account, with no per-lead fees.

How long does an HVAC build take?

The replacement quote funnel, seasonal ad setup, follow-up automation and job-value tracking are live in 5 to 10 business days. If you want to be ready for peak season, start the build a few weeks before demand rises so the campaigns have time to learn before the busiest weeks arrive.

Who owns the leads and the system?

You do. The funnel, CRM, automations, ad account and every lead belong to the HVAC company, not to us or a marketplace. Leads are exclusive, and if you stop working with us the system keeps running, so the data and audience history you build each season stay with your business.
