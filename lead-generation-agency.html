Quick answer

What does a lead generation agency do?

A lead generation agency finds and converts potential customers for your business. A full-service one plans the offer, runs paid ads, builds the landing page or form, verifies and qualifies each lead, follows up instantly and books appointments into your calendar. The best ones report on booked calls and revenue, not only on cost per lead.

01

How our lead generation system works

  1. Audit and offer. We look at your market, current cost per lead, close rate and what a customer is worth, then agree the offer and the target cost per booked call.
  2. Funnel build. A landing page, quiz or OTP-verified instant form, with tracking and the Conversions API connected before launch.
  3. Ads launch. A testing campaign for new creative and a scaling campaign for winners, run from your own ad account through our Facebook ads agency service.
  4. Instant follow-up. Speed-to-lead automation texts and calls each lead within 60 seconds, and an AI appointment setter can qualify and book after hours.
  5. Booking and reminders. Calls are booked into your calendar with reminders, and every stage is visible in one CRM pipeline.
  6. Weekly optimization. We report spend, leads, contact rate, bookings, shows and revenue, and change creative, offer or form based on cost per booked call.
02

Pay per lead, retainer or done-for-you build?

Lead generation agencies charge in three main ways. Each one rewards the agency for something different, so the model matters as much as the price.

ModelHow it worksWatch out for
Pay per leadYou pay a fixed price for each lead deliveredLeads are often shared with competitors, and the agency is paid for volume rather than quality
Monthly retainerA management fee for running ads and campaignsReports that stop at clicks and cost per lead, with nobody owning what happens after
Build plus management (our model)A one-time funnel and automation build, then ads management quoted for your accountNeeds your team to answer and close the calls we book

Our funnel builds start at $999 for most offers and run to $1,999 for multi-step builds with automation. Ad spend is paid by you straight to Meta, so you own the account, pixel data and leads. Exclusive leads that come from your own ads tend to close far better than shared leads bought per unit. Compare the economics with the cost per lead calculator.

04

How to choose a lead generation agency

Ask these questions on the first call. The answers tell you more than a portfolio does.

  • Are the leads exclusive to me? Shared leads are cheaper because three competitors are calling the same person.
  • Whose ad account and data is it? You should own the ad account, pixel, audiences and CRM.
  • What happens in the first five minutes after a lead comes in? If the answer is "your team calls them", ask what happens at 9 pm on a Sunday.
  • What do you report on? Look for booked calls, show rate and revenue, not only impressions, clicks and cost per lead.
  • Can I see real campaign screenshots? Ask for dashboards with dates, spend and results, like the ones on our case studies page.

Not sure an agency is right for you yet? Our funnel agency vs freelancer comparison covers the trade-offs, and a sales funnel audit shows where your current leads are leaking.

05

What to expect in the first 90 days

Days 1 to 10: offer agreed, funnel and tracking built, follow-up automation tested, ads approved. Days 10 to 30: first leads usually arrive within days of launch; we test creative and form types and fix where leads drop off. Days 30 to 90: winning ads move into scaling, and the target becomes a stable cost per booked call you can plan around.

Results depend on your market, offer, budget and how quickly your team works the calendar, so we do not promise a number of leads before seeing your account. What we do commit to is reporting every week on the numbers that pay you: booked calls, shows and revenue. Book a call for an honest view of what is realistic in your market.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a lead generation agency cost?

It depends on the model. Pay-per-lead vendors charge per lead, often from a few dollars to over a hundred depending on the industry. Retainer agencies charge a monthly management fee. Our funnel builds start at $999, ads management is quoted after we see your account, and your ad spend goes straight to Meta from your own account.

Do you sell shared or exclusive leads?

Exclusive. We do not sell lead lists. Every lead comes from ads running in your own ad account to your own funnel, so it belongs to you and no competitor receives the same contact. That is the main reason exclusive leads usually close at a much higher rate than bought, shared leads.

How fast will I get leads?

Most lead campaigns produce first leads within days of launch once ads are approved. A Texas roofing client received 686 leads at $5.31 each in the first eight days. Reaching a stable cost per booked call usually takes several weeks of creative and offer testing, and we report on it weekly.

Which industries do you work with?

Mostly service businesses that sell through a call or appointment: roofing, HVAC, solar, mortgage, insurance, personal injury, med spas, real estate, coaching, consulting and agencies. We also run eCommerce campaigns. If your sale needs a conversation, our system of instant follow-up and booking is built for you.

What do I need to provide?

Access to your ad account (or we help you create one), your calendar, a clear service area and offer, and someone on your team who takes the booked calls. We handle the funnel, ads, tracking, CRM and follow-up automation, and we write the ad copy and scripts with you.

Do you guarantee results?

We do not guarantee a number of leads or sales, because results depend on your market, offer, budget and sales team. We do guarantee transparency: you own the accounts, see the same dashboards we see, and get a weekly report on spend, leads, bookings, shows and revenue.
