About these results

Frequently asked questions.

What results do Funnels That Print case studies show?

Funnels That Print has documented 275+ client results since 2019 across lead generation and eCommerce. Highlights include roofing leads from $4.27, 8,810 MVA leads at $7.61, a $1.52M high-ticket consulting funnel, an $847,307 low-ticket funnel and a $2,300,000 DTC scale at 11.6x ROAS. Every figure comes from client ad accounts or CRMs.

How many case studies are listed here?

275+ verified client results since 2019: 125+ lead generation funnels and 126+ eCommerce and DTC campaigns, each with real screenshots and numbers. Cases are grouped into industry hubs such as roofing, MVA, mortgage, coaching and Shopify, so you can jump straight to results from businesses similar to yours.

Which industries do the case studies cover?

Roofing, solar, HVAC, mortgage, medspa, dental, legal, coaching and consulting, real estate, local trades, and DTC brands in beauty, apparel, supplements and fitness. Low-ticket info products, agencies and SaaS offers are covered too. Each industry hub summarizes typical cost per lead or ROAS and links to every case in that niche.

Are the results verified?

Each case shows ad account or CRM screenshots with cost per lead, booked calls, return on ad spend or collected revenue as reported in the client's own platforms. We publish the figures as recorded rather than averaging them, so ranges like $4.27 to $24 per roofing lead reflect real campaigns in different markets.

How do I get similar results for my business?

Book a strategy call. We take the closest case to your offer, map its funnel, automation and tracking stack to your business, and build it in 5 to 10 business days. Results depend on your offer, market and budget, so we use the closest documented cases to set realistic targets before launch.

How much does a funnel build cost?

Builds are a one-time fee rather than a retainer: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and runs through your own accounts, which you keep.

Which case studies are the biggest results?

Two have their own full breakdowns: a high-ticket consulting funnel that collected $1.52M from cold traffic, and a low-ticket funnel that collected $847,307 with a $27 offer and a $17 order bump. In eCommerce, the largest documented scale is $2,300,000 at 11.6x return on ad spend.
