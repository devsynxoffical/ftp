About the $847K build

Frequently asked questions.

What is a self-liquidating offer funnel?

A funnel that sells a low-priced front-end product, here $27 with a $17 order bump, so front-end sales cover the ad spend while building a list of buyers for higher-ticket backend offers. Because acquisition pays for itself, the business can scale ad spend without waiting months for backend revenue to arrive.

How much of the $847,307 came from the front end?

The front end recovered 112% of ad spend on day zero, but most of the collected revenue came from the backend ascension sequence sold to the buyer list. The front end's job was to acquire buyers at break-even or better; the email and SMS sequences that followed turned those buyers into the bulk of the $847,307.

What average order value does an SLO funnel need?

Average order value has to exceed cost per buyer. This funnel ran at $68.40 AOV, driven by the order bump and a one-click upsell after checkout. With a $27 front-end price, the bump and upsell more than doubled what each buyer spent, which is what allowed the front end to self-liquidate.

Is a low-ticket funnel right for my business?

It fits info products, coaching, software and eCommerce offers that have a backend to sell. For high-ticket local services, an application funnel with AI lead qualification usually performs better. A good test: if buyers of a $7 to $97 product would naturally want your higher-priced offer next, a low-ticket front end can work.

What does a low ticket funnel agency do?

A low ticket funnel agency builds the full front-end system: the offer, sales page, checkout, order bump, one-click upsell, buyer email and SMS sequences and purchase tracking. Funnels That Print builds that stack in 5 to 10 business days for a one-time fee of $999, $1,499 or $1,999, with no revenue share.

Can you build a self-liquidating offer funnel for my business?

Yes. As an SLO funnel builder we plan the front-end product, price, order bump and upsell, then build the sales page, checkout, buyer follow-up and purchase tracking, usually in 5 to 10 business days from $999. We model average order value against your expected cost per buyer before launch, so you know what the front end must do to cover ad spend.
