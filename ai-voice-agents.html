Quick answer

What does an AI voice agent do for lead qualification?

It phones each new lead seconds after they submit a form, confirms who they are, discloses it is an AI assistant, asks two to four qualifying questions such as budget, timeline and situation, answers common questions, then books qualified leads into an open slot and logs the outcome and a call summary in your CRM.

01

What we set up

  1. Trigger. The form or ad lead fires a webhook from your CRM to the voice platform.
  2. Script. A four-part structure: confirm and anchor to the form, disclose, qualify, offer two slots. Our AI voice agent script template shows it in full.
  3. Knowledge base. 30 to 60 approved answers about the offer, pricing ranges, process and timing.
  4. Calendar. Live availability with round-robin across reps.
  5. Handoff rules. Warm transfer during business hours or a booked callback when the prospect asks for a person.
  6. Write-back. Qualification answers, objections, booked slot and a two-line summary saved to the CRM.
02

The three things that decide whether prospects stay on the call

  • Latency under 700 milliseconds between the prospect finishing a sentence and the agent replying. Above a second, people talk over the agent or hang up.
  • A clear, early disclosure that the caller is an AI assistant, followed by a reason to continue.
  • Interruption handling: the agent stops talking when the prospect speaks.

We cover latency, voices and objection handling in detail in AI appointment setter cost and setup.

Frequently asked questions.

How quickly does the AI agent call a new lead?

Within about 60 seconds of the form submission during allowed calling hours, at the same moment an SMS goes out. Outside allowed hours the text is sent immediately and the call is queued for the morning. Unanswered calls retry after 10 minutes and again after two hours.

What does an AI voice agent cost?

Platform, telephony and speech costs together usually run $0.10 to $0.25 per minute of talk time. A qualifying call averages two to four minutes, which at typical contact and booking rates works out to about $3 to $9 per booked appointment, plus the one-time build.

Can the AI handle objections?

Yes, within limits. Each common objection, such as price, timing or 'just looking', gets a short approved answer that acknowledges it, responds from the knowledge base and returns to booking. Anything outside the knowledge base triggers the handoff rule rather than an improvised answer.

Do I need to tell people they are speaking to an AI?

We always disclose it in the first lines of the call. It is the honest approach, many jurisdictions require it, and in our experience clear disclosure followed by a quick, useful conversation keeps more prospects on the line than trying to pass as human.

How do you test the agent before launch?

We run at least 50 internal test calls covering normal bookings, objections, interruptions, wrong numbers and requests for a human, then fix the script and knowledge base before the agent touches a real lead. After launch we review transcripts weekly for the first month.
