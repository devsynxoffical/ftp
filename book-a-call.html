About the call

Frequently asked questions.

Is the funnel strategy call really free?

Yes. The call is free and there is no obligation to buy. We use the 30 minutes to understand your offer, look at your current funnel and numbers, and recommend the first thing to fix. If we are a fit, you get a fixed quote and timeline in writing after the call.

Who will I speak with on the call?

Gaurav or Rahul Kapoor, the founders of Funnels That Print. They have built and reviewed the funnels behind our 275+ documented client results, including the $1.52M consulting funnel and the $847K low-ticket front end, so you speak with the people who would plan your build.

What should I prepare before the call?

Have your offer and price, your current funnel or landing page link, roughly how many leads you get per month, and your cost per lead or cost per booked call if you track it. Ad account access is not needed. If you have none of this yet, book anyway and we will start from the offer.

What happens after the call?

Within one business day you receive a short plan with the recommended funnel, automations and tracking, a fixed price from $999 to $1,999 depending on scope, and a timeline. Most builds go live in 5 to 10 business days. You decide whether to go ahead; there is no follow-up pressure.

I can't find a time that suits me. What now?

Message us on WhatsApp or email info@funnelsthatprint.com with two or three times that work in your time zone and we will fit you in. We work with clients in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE, so early-morning and evening slots are usually possible.
