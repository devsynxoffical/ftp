Roofing Lead Generation Funnel: The Cost-Per-Lead Playbook for Exclusive Roofing Leads

Stop buying shared leads. Here is the quote estimator page, ad angles, 60-second follow-up and tracking setup that produce exclusive roofing leads at a cost that makes the math work.

Gaurav KapoorFounder & Funnel Strategist, Funnels That Print
Key takeaways
  • The highest-converting roofing funnel is a 4-step quote estimator (roof type, issue, timeline, address) on a fast page, not a contact form. It converts two to three times better on cold traffic.
  • Roofing leads go cold in minutes. Instant SMS, an AI or human call inside 60 seconds and missed-call text-back are what turn a $20 to $60 lead into a booked inspection.
  • Exclusive leads from your own funnel cost a fraction of shared leads from marketplaces and close at a far higher rate, because the homeowner only spoke to you.
  • Send booked-inspection and signed-contract events back to Meta and Google so the ads optimize for jobs, not form fills.

1. Why Roofing Leads Are Expensive (and Shared Leads Make It Worse)

Roofing lead generation is one of the most competitive corners of home services. A replacement job is worth $10,000 to $40,000, so every roofer in the area bids on the same clicks, and the lead marketplaces sell the same homeowner to three to five companies at once. That is why a shared replacement lead can cost well over $100 and still close at single-digit rates.

The way out is to own the funnel. A page you control, ads you run, and a follow-up system that reaches the homeowner before anyone else does. The result is an exclusive lead: one homeowner, one roofer, one conversation. We have built this funnel for roofing, solar, HVAC and other trades across our 125+ lead generation case studies, and the structure is the same every time. Every roofing result is collected on the roofing lead generation case studies page, and the same system for heating and cooling is on the HVAC case studies page.

2. The 4-Step Quote Estimator Page

The single biggest conversion lever is replacing the contact form with a short quote estimator. The homeowner answers four questions, one per screen, and gets a next step. On cold traffic this converts two to three times better than a form, for the same reason a diagnostic quiz beats a static page: each small answer builds commitment before the contact details are asked for.

  1. What type of roof do you have? Shingle, metal, tile, flat. Pictures, not words.
  2. What's going on? Leak or damage, storm or hail, roof is old, just getting quotes.
  3. When do you need it done? ASAP, within a month, this year, not sure.
  4. Where's the property? Address with autocomplete, then name and mobile number, with the consent line for a call and text.

The page itself: one headline that names the service area and the offer ("Free roof inspection in [City], quote in 24 hours"), three trust elements (years in business, reviews, licence and insurance), a before-and-after strip, and the estimator above the fold on mobile. Everything loads in under two seconds. The answers route the lead: storm damage and leaks go to the same-day queue, "just getting quotes" goes to a nurture sequence.

3. Ad Angles: Storm, Age, Insurance and Financing

01

Storm and hail

Geo-targeted within days of a weather event. "Hail hit [County] on Tuesday. Free inspection before you file." Highest urgency, highest volume, short window.

02

Roof age

Target homeowners in neighbourhoods built 18 to 30 years ago. "Roofs installed in the 2000s are reaching end of life. Know before it leaks."

03

Insurance claims

"We handle the claim paperwork with you." Educational angle that pre-frames the homeowner to use you as the contractor of record.

04

Financing

"New roof from $X per month, 0% for 12 months." Converts the deferred buyer who knows the roof is due but fears the lump sum.

Creative that works is local and specific: the owner on camera, a real crew on a real roof in the service area, and a drone shot of a finished job. Stock photos of generic roofs underperform every time.

4. Meta and Google Campaign Structure

Meta: one campaign per angle, radius or county targeting around the service area, broad audiences with the estimator conversion event as the optimization goal once it has volume. Lead-form ads inside Meta produce cheaper but much lower-quality leads; sending traffic to the estimator page costs more per lead and far less per job.

Google search: exact and phrase match on "roof repair [city]", "roofing company near me", "roof replacement cost" and the storm terms during events, with call extensions and a call-only campaign for mobile. Google leads cost more per click and convert at a higher rate, and they arrive with intent, so the follow-up speed in the next section matters even more.

Local Services Ads and Google Business Profile run alongside for the branded and "near me" demand. Retarget every estimator visitor who did not finish with the financing and insurance angles.

5. The 60-Second Follow-Up That Books the Inspection

A roofing lead has a shelf life measured in minutes. The homeowner has usually submitted two or three requests, and the first company to call books the inspection. The system that wins is the 60-second speed-to-lead stack:

  • Instant SMS from the office number: "Hi [Name], it's [Owner] from [Company]. Got your request for [Street]. Calling you in a minute, or reply here with a good time."
  • Immediate call from a rep during hours, or an AI appointment setter that confirms the issue, asks about timing and books the inspection slot on the crew calendar, around the clock.
  • Missed-call text-back on the inbound line, because half of roofing leads call rather than fill in a form.
  • Inspection reminders the day before and the morning of, plus a photo of the technician, which cuts no-shows the same way it does for sales calls.
  • A 5-day sequence for unreached leads and a 90-day sequence for "just getting quotes", with the financing and insurance content.

Work backwards from the job. If an average replacement is $15,000 at a 30% gross margin, a job is worth $4,500 in margin. At a 20% close rate from booked inspections and a 60% inspection show rate, you can afford roughly $500 per booked inspection and still make money, which means a $60 lead that books one inspection in three is comfortably profitable. The point of the funnel is to make those rates predictable rather than chasing the cheapest lead.

7. Tracking Jobs, Not Form Fills

If the ads optimize for form submissions, Meta and Google will find you cheap, low-intent leads. Send the events that matter back instead: estimator completed, inspection booked, inspection attended, contract signed, with the job value. That is the CRM event ladder from our Meta Conversions API guide, and after about two weeks the ad platforms start delivering homeowners who look like the ones who signed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good cost per lead for roofing?

It depends on the market and the season, but exclusive leads from your own funnel typically land between $20 and $80 on Meta and $50 to $150 on Google search, against $100 to $300 or more for a replacement-job lead from a shared marketplace. Our best home services campaign ran a verified $4.90 cost per lead over 12 months with a repair-and-inspection offer.

Do Facebook ads work for roofing leads?

Yes, when the ad leads to a quote estimator rather than a contact form and the follow-up happens inside a minute. Storm, roof age and financing angles perform best. Google search converts at a higher rate per click but costs more per click; most roofers run both.

How fast do I need to follow up with a roofing lead?

Inside 60 seconds. Homeowners usually submit two or three requests in a row. The first company to call books the inspection. An instant SMS plus an immediate call, with missed-call text-back for inbound, is the system that wins that race.

Should I buy shared roofing leads or build a funnel?

Shared leads are useful to fill gaps, but every lead is also sold to three to five competitors, so close rates are low and price pressure is high. A funnel you own produces exclusive leads at a lower cost per job and builds a retargeting audience and email list you keep.
