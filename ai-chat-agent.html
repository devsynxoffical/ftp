01

Why messages need an instant reply

A prospect who sends a DM or texts back is at the same point as someone who just filled in a form: interested now, and probably asking competitors too. Across 60+ of our speed-to-lead deployments, contact rate was 84% when leads were reached in the first minute, the median first contact was 38 seconds and lead-to-booking rates rose 2.4x on the same ads. Those figures come from form and call follow-up, but the principle is the same for chat: the first useful reply usually wins. The background is in what is speed to lead and our speed to lead statistics.

03

How a chat conversation runs

  1. Greet and anchor: reply to what the person actually asked, not a generic welcome.
  2. Qualify: ask two to four questions from your lead qualification questions, one at a time.
  3. Answer: respond only from the approved knowledge base. Unknown answers become a handoff, never a guess.
  4. Book: offer two specific times from the live calendar, then confirm by SMS and email.
  5. Hand off: pricing negotiations, complaints, sensitive topics or "can I talk to someone?" go straight to your team with a summary.
04

The knowledge base is the product

A chat agent is only as good as what it is allowed to say. We write approved answers covering services, process, service area, typical timelines, price ranges you are comfortable sharing and what happens on the call. You review every answer before launch. Anything not in the knowledge base is out of bounds: the agent says it will check with the team and creates a task. That rule is what stops a chatbot from inventing a discount or a guarantee. When your offer, prices or hours change, the knowledge base is updated once, so every channel gives the same answer. Copy for the follow-up messages comes from our SMS follow-up templates.

05

Where chat agents fit best

Chat matters most where prospects message before they call: med spas answering treatment questions from Instagram, real estate teams replying to listing inquiries, coaches running DM campaigns and home services companies that get texts after hours. If most of your leads call instead, the AI receptionist is a better first step, and if they fill in forms, start with the AI voice agent. All options are compared on our AI agents page.

Frequently asked questions.

Is an AI chat agent the same as a website chatbot?

It covers more ground. A website chatbot lives in one widget and often follows buttons. Our chat agent answers free-form questions across website chat, SMS, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp, uses one knowledge base for all of them, books calls on your live calendar and writes everything back to the same contact record in your CRM.

Can the chat agent answer pricing questions?

Only the way you approve. Many clients share a starting price or range and explain what changes it, and the agent sticks to that wording. If someone asks for a custom quote, a discount or anything outside the knowledge base, the agent hands the conversation to your team instead of guessing.

What happens when someone wants a human?

The agent stops and routes the thread to the right person, with a short summary of what the prospect asked and answered. During business hours your team gets an instant notification; after hours the agent explains when someone will reply and can book a call slot so the lead is not left waiting.

Does it work with Instagram and WhatsApp?

Yes, through the CRM's connected channels, as long as the accounts are business accounts linked to the CRM and the messaging platforms' policies are followed. WhatsApp has its own rules for templates and for messaging people outside an open conversation window, which we set up during the build.

Which AI powers the chat agent?

We use GoHighLevel Conversation AI when the use case fits its built-in features, because it is simplest to maintain. When a client needs more complex logic or tighter control over prompts, we connect another AI model to the CRM. Either way the knowledge base, booking and handoff rules work the same.

How do you stop the bot from saying something wrong?

Three controls. The agent answers only from an approved knowledge base, it hands off on any topic it is not cleared for, and we review conversation logs every week during the first month. When a question comes up repeatedly, we add an approved answer rather than letting the agent improvise.
