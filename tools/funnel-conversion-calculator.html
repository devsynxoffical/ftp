Sales funnel calculator: conversion rate, revenue and ROAS

This sales funnel calculator, also called a funnel conversion calculator, follows a visitor all the way to a sale: landing page opt-in, booked call, attended call and closed deal. Enter your traffic, ad spend and the rate at each step, and it returns leads, sales, cost per lead, cost per attended call, cost per sale, revenue and ROAS so you can see which stage is costing you the most.

Quick answer

How do you calculate funnel conversion rate?

Multiply the rate at each stage. Leads equal visitors times opt-in rate, booked calls equal leads times booking rate, attended calls equal booked times show rate, and sales equal attended times close rate. Visitor-to-sale conversion is sales divided by visitors. With 8%, 30%, 70% and 20%, that is 0.34%.

01

How to use the calculator

  1. Visitors and ad spend. Use landing page sessions and spend for the same month, not ad clicks, which overcount.
  2. Opt-in rate. Leads divided by landing page visitors.
  3. Booking rate. Booked calls divided by leads. If you sell at checkout, set booking and show rate to 100% and use close rate as checkout conversion.
  4. Show-up rate. Attended calls divided by booked calls. See the show rate definition.
  5. Close rate and deal value. Sales divided by attended calls, and average first-payment or contract value.

Worked example with the default numbers

The calculator opens with 5,000 visitors, $5,000 of ad spend, an 8% opt-in rate, 30% booking rate, 70% show rate, 20% close rate and a $3,000 deal:

Visitor-to-sale conversion is 0.34%. Now test one lever at a time. Raising show rate from 70% to 85% gives 102 attended calls, 20.4 sales and $61,200 of revenue on the same spend, a bigger gain than most landing page redesigns deliver.

03

How to read the results

Look at cost per attended call and cost per sale before ROAS. ROAS on a high-ticket offer looks healthy even with a broken stage, because one deal covers a lot of spend. The stage with the biggest drop against benchmark is where the next dollar of effort pays most. Compare cost per sale with gross profit per sale: if cost per sale is above it, the funnel loses money no matter how good the ROAS figure looks.

05

What to fix when a number is bad

  • Low opt-in rate: the offer or page. A quiz funnel or VSL funnel usually beats a long form. Start from the funnel copy template.
  • Low booking rate: slow follow-up. Install speed-to-lead automation so every lead is contacted inside a minute.
  • Low show rate: reminders. Use the no-show reminder sequence and price the gap with the no-show cost calculator.
  • Low close rate: lead quality. Add qualification questions or an AI voice agent that screens before the closer.

Frequently asked questions.

What is a good sales funnel conversion rate?

There is no single number, because it depends on offer price and traffic temperature. For a high-ticket call funnel, visitor-to-sale rates under 1% are normal and can be very profitable. Judge the funnel by cost per sale against gross profit per sale rather than by the conversion percentage alone.

Should I use ad clicks or landing page visitors?

Landing page visitors. Ad platforms count link clicks, and many of those never load the page, especially on mobile. Using clicks makes your opt-in rate look worse than it is. Pull sessions from your analytics or funnel builder for the same date range as your ad spend.

How do I use it for a checkout funnel with no calls?

Set the booking rate and show rate to 100% so every lead passes straight through, then enter checkout conversion as the close rate and average order value as the deal value. The ROAS calculator is a simpler option if you already know revenue and spend.

Which stage should I fix first?

Fix the stage furthest below its benchmark that sits closest to revenue. A 15-point gain in show rate or close rate usually beats a similar gain at the top, because it applies to leads you already paid for. Change one input at a time in the calculator to compare the revenue impact.

Why does my ROAS look good when I am not profitable?

ROAS ignores delivery costs, refunds, sales commissions and the margin on the product. A 3x ROAS on a 30% margin offer loses money. Run the ROAS calculator with your gross margin to see the break-even ROAS, then compare it to the ROAS this calculator gives you.
