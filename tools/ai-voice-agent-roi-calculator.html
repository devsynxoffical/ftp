How to use the calculator

  1. New leads per month and the human setter cost, including salary, commission and tools.
  2. Human contact rate: share of leads your team actually speaks to today.
  3. AI contact rate: share an AI reaches by calling within 60 seconds and retrying. Our speed-to-lead deployments reach 84% inside the first minute.

How to read the results

Look at extra bookings first, then cost per booking. Extra bookings come from contact rate, which is mostly speed. If your human team already calls inside a minute around the clock, the gap shrinks and the case rests on cost. How much does an AI appointment setter cost?

An AI appointment setter has two costs: usage and the build.

  • Usage: platform, telephony and speech costs land at about $0.10 to $0.25 per minute. A qualifying call averages two to four minutes, so roughly $3 to $9 per booked appointment at the contact and booking rates we see.
  • Build: script, CRM connection, calendar booking, testing and compliance setup. Our complete suite, which includes the AI booking agent with the funnel, CRM, reminders and tracking, is a one-time $1,999.
  • Upkeep: scripts need updates when offers, hours or calendars change, which the calculator includes as a monthly figure.

Frequently asked questions.

How much does an AI voice agent cost per minute?

In our deployments, AI calling runs about $0.10 to $0.25 per minute depending on the voice, model and telephony provider. The calculator default is $0.15. Add a monthly figure for building, testing and maintaining the agent, because scripts and integrations need updates as offers and calendars change.

Will an AI voice agent replace my setters?

It usually replaces the first call, not the whole team. The AI handles instant contact, qualification and booking around the clock, then hands warm or complex conversations to people. Many clients redeploy setters to confirmation calls, reactivation and closing, where human judgment earns more per hour.

Why is the AI contact rate higher than the human rate?

Speed. An AI calls within seconds of the form, while the lead is still on their phone and thinking about you. Human teams call minutes or hours later and miss nights and weekends. Across our deployments, 84% of leads were reached inside the first minute.

Do I need to disclose that callers are talking to AI?

Yes. Identify the agent as an AI assistant at the start of the call, get consent to call and text at opt-in, and honor opt-out requests immediately. Rules vary by state and country and change often, so confirm requirements with counsel before you launch outbound calling.

What booking rate should I assume?

Use the booking rate your team gets today on contacted leads, and keep it the same for the AI to start. That isolates the effect of speed and coverage. After a few weeks of live calls, replace the AI figure with real data, which can land above or below your human rate.
