About these results

Frequently asked questions.

What do the eCommerce case studies show?

Return on ad spend, average order value, cost per purchase and scaling results for DTC brands on Meta ads, with screenshots from the ad account and store. Cases range from small tests of a few thousand dollars to stores scaling past $1,000,000, including Q4 and Black Friday pushes.

Which eCommerce niches are included?

Beauty and skincare, apparel, supplements, fitness equipment, home goods and direct-response dropshipping stores. Each niche has its own hub page summarizing typical ROAS and the creative and offer structure that worked, so you can compare results from brands with similar margins, price points and buying cycles to yours.

What return on ad spend is realistic?

It depends on margin and average order value. Direct-response landing pages, order bumps and post-purchase upsells typically lift AOV enough to make a 2x to 4x ROAS profitable. Most documented scaling cases here run between roughly 4x and 5x, with a top result of 11.6x on a $2,300,000 scale.

Do you build the store or only the funnel?

We build the funnel pages, checkout flow, upsells and post-purchase follow-up, connect tracking through Meta CAPI, and integrate with Shopify and other store platforms. Your existing store, products and fulfillment stay in place. In our setups, server-side tracking recovered 31% more purchase events than the browser pixel alone.

How much does an eCommerce funnel build cost?

Builds are a one-time fee: $999 for design only, $1,499 for design, copy and the full build, or $1,999 for the complete suite with CRM, automation, an AI booking agent and tracking. Ad spend is separate and stays in your own Meta account, so budget and data remain under your control.

How fast can an eCommerce funnel launch?

Landing pages, offer structure, upsells and server-side tracking are live in 5 to 10 business days. Scaling speed then depends on creative testing. Some documented launches moved quickly, such as $105,000 in 5 days and $126,000 in 14 days, but most brands need a few weeks of testing before budgets increase.
